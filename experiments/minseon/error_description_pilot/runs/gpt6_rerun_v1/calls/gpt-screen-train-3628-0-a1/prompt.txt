QUESTION: Edmund is buying a new computer and needs to save up $75 before he has enough. He convinces his parents to pay him for extra chores. He normally has to do 12 chores a week. His parents agree to pay him $2 for every extra chore he does during the week. If he does 4 chores a day for two weeks, how much does he earn?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of chores Edmund does in two weeks.
Edmund does 4 chores a day for two weeks.
Total chores in two weeks = 4 chores/day * 7 days/week * 2 weeks = 56 chores

Step 2: Calculate the number of extra chores Edmund does.
Extra chores = Total chores - Normal chores
Extra chores = 56 chores - 12 chores = 44 chores

Step 3: Calculate the total amount Edmund earns.
Edmund earns $2 for each extra chore.
Total earnings = Extra chores * Earnings per chore
Total earnings = 44 chores * $2/chores = $88
