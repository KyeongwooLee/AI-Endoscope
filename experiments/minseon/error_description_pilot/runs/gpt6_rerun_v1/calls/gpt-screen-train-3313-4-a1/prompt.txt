QUESTION: Marco uses a fifth of an ounce of dry tea leaves to brew his morning cup of tea each day. He buys tea leaves in boxes of 28 ounces. How many weeks of daily tea does Marco get from a box?
SOLUTION: #### 8
Marco uses 1/5 of an ounce of tea leaves per cup of tea.
He buys tea leaves in boxes of 28 ounces.

To find out how many weeks of tea Marco gets from a box, we first calculate how many cups of tea he can brew from one box:
\[ \text{Cups per box} = \frac{28 \text{ ounces}}{1/5 \text{ ounces per cup}} \]

\[ \text{Cups per box} = 28 \times 5 \]

\[ \text{Cups per box} = 140 \text{ cups} \]

Since Marco uses one cup of tea per day, we divide the total number of cups by the number of days in a week to find out how many weeks of tea he can get from the box:
\[ \text{Weeks of tea} = \frac{140 \text{ cups}}{7 \text{ days per week}} \]

\[ \text{Weeks of tea} = 20 \]
