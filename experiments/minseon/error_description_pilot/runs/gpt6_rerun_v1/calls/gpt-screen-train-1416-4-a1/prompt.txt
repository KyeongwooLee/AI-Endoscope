QUESTION: The company's data entry team had 5 employees working on a large project.  Rudy types 64 words per minute, Joyce types 76 words per minute, Gladys types 91 words per minute, Lisa types 80 words per minute and Mike types 89 words per minute.  What is the team's average typed words per minute?
SOLUTION: To find the team's average typed words per minute, we first need to calculate the total number of words typed by all employees. Then we divide this total by the number of employees.

1. Calculate the total number of words typed by each employee:
   - Rudy: \( 64 \text{ words per minute} \times 10 \text{ minutes} = 640 \text{ words} \)
   - Joyce: \( 76 \text{ words per minute} \times 10 \text{ minutes} = 760 \text{ words} \)
   - Gladys: \( 91 \text{ words per minute} \times 10 \text{ minutes} = 910 \text{ words} \)
   - Lisa: \( 80 \text{ words per minute} \times 10 \text{ minutes} = 800 \text{ words} \)
   - Mike: \( 89 \text{ words per minute} \times 10 \text{ minutes} = 890 \text{ words} \)

2. Add up all the words typed by the employees:
   \( 640 + 760 + 910 + 800 + 890 = 4000 \text{ words} \)

3. Divide the total number of words by the number of employees to find the average:
   \( \frac{4000 \text{ words}}{5 \text{ employees}} = 800 \text{ words per minute} \)

#### 800
The team's average typed words per minute is 800.
