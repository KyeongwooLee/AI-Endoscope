QUESTION: James gets a fleet of gas transportation vans.  He gets 6 vans.  2 of them are 8000 gallons.  1 of them is 30% less than that.  The remaining trucks are 50% larger than the 2 trucks.  How many gallons can he transport?

30% less than 8000 gallons = 8000 * 0.30 = 2400 gallons less.
So, 8000 - 2400 = 5600 gallons for the third van.

Step 2: Calculate the size of the remaining two vans.
50% larger than 8000 gallons = 8000 * 1.50 = 12000 gallons.
So, 12000 gallons for each of the remaining two vans.

2 vans * 8000 gallons = 16000 gallons.
1 van * 5600 gallons = 5600 gallons.
2 vans * 12000 gallons = 24000 gallons.

Step 4: Add the sizes together.
