QUESTION: Bobby has three squares of fabric that he will turn into a flag. The first square is 8 feet by 5 feet. The second one is 10 feet by 7 feet. The third one is 5 feet by 5 feet. If he wants his flag to be 15 feet long, how tall will it be?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total area of the fabric.
Area of first square = 8 feet * 5 feet = 40 square feet
Area of second square = 10 feet * 7 feet = 70 square feet
Area of third square = 5 feet * 5 feet = 25 square feet
Total area = 40 + 70 + 25 = 135 square feet

Step 2: Determine the number of square feet per foot of height.
Since the total area is 135 square feet and the flag is 15 feet long, the number of square feet per foot of height is 135 / 15 = 9 square feet per foot

Step 3: Calculate the total height of the flag.
Total height = Total area / Number of square feet per foot
Total height = 135 square feet / 9 square feet per foot = 15 feet
