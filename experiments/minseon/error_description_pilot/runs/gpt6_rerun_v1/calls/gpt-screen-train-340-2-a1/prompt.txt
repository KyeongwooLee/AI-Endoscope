QUESTION: Tom needs to lower a rope down 6 stories.   One story is 10 feet.  The only rope being sold is 20 feet long but you lose 25% when lashing them together.  How many pieces of rope will he need to buy?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total length of rope needed
Total length needed = Number of stories * Length of one story
Total length needed = 6 * 10 = 60 feet

Step 2: Calculate the length of rope after losing 25%
Length of rope after losing 25% = Total length needed * (1 - 0.25)
Length of rope after losing 25% = 60 * 0.75 = 45 feet

Step 3: Calculate the number of pieces needed
Number of pieces needed = Length of rope after losing 25% / Length of one piece
Number of pieces needed = 45 / 20 = 2.25

Since Tom cannot buy a fraction of a piece of rope, he needs to round up to the nearest whole number.
