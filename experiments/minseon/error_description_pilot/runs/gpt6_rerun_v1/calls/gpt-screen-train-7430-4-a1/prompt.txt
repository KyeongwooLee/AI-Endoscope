QUESTION: A park has 50 benches with a capacity of 4 people each. On a Sunday afternoon, 80 people were sitting on the benches. What's the number of available spaces on the benches that other people can sit on before the carrying capacity of the benches is full?
SOLUTION: #### 20
To determine the number of available spaces on the benches, we first need to calculate the total capacity of the benches. Given that there are 50 benches with a capacity of 4 people each:

Total capacity = Number of benches × Capacity per bench
Total capacity = 50 benches × 4 people/bench
Total capacity = 200 people

Next, we know that on a Sunday afternoon, 80 people were sitting on the benches. To find the number of available spaces, we subtract the number of people sitting from the total capacity:

Available spaces = Total capacity - Number of people sitting
Available spaces = 200 people - 80 people
Available spaces = 120 people

Thus, there are 120 available spaces on the benches that other people can sit on before the carrying capacity is full.
