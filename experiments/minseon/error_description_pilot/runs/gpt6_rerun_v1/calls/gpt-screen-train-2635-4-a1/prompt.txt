QUESTION: John and his two brothers decide to split the cost of an apartment.  It is 40% more expensive than John's old apartment which costs $1200 per month.  How much does John save per year by splitting the apartment compared to living alone?
SOLUTION: Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Calculate the cost of the new apartment:**
   - John's old apartment costs $1200 per month.
   - The new apartment is 40% more expensive than John's old apartment.
   - To find the cost increase, we calculate 40% of $1200.
     \[
     40\% \text{ of } \$1200 = 0.40 \times 1200 = \$480
     \]
   - Add this increase to the cost of John's old apartment to find the cost of the new apartment.
     \[
     \text{Cost of new apartment} = \$1200 + \$480 = \$1680 \text{ per month}
     \]

2. **Calculate the total cost for John if he lived alone:**
   - If John lived alone, he would pay the full cost of the new apartment.
   - Therefore, the total cost for John would be $1680 per month.

3. **Calculate the total cost if John splits the cost with his brothers:**
   - John and his two brothers split the cost equally.
   - The total cost for three people would be three times the cost for one person.
   - Therefore, the total cost per month for John with his brothers would be:
     \[
     \text{Total cost per month} = \frac{\$1680}{3} = \$560 \text{ per month}
     \]

4. **Calculate the monthly savings for John:**
   - Subtract the monthly cost with his brothers from the monthly cost if he lived alone.
     \[
     \text{Monthly savings} = \$1680 - \$560 = \$1120
     \]

5. **Calculate the annual savings for John:**
   - There are 12 months in a year, so multiply the monthly savings by 12.
     \[
     \text{Annual savings} = \$1120 \times 12 = \$13440
     \]

#### 13440
Therefore, John saves $13,440 per year by splitting the apartment with his brothers.
