QUESTION: If it takes 8 days for 3 builders to build a cottage. How long will it take 6 builders to build the same size cottage working at the same rate?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the total amount of work done by the builders in one day.
Since 3 builders can build the cottage in 8 days, we can say that 3 builders can complete the cottage in 8 days.

Step 2: Calculate the work rate of 3 builders.
The total amount of work is 1 cottage, and it's done by 3 builders in 8 days.
So, the work rate of 3 builders is 1 cottage / (3 builders * 8 days).

Step 3: Calculate the total work done by 3 builders in 1 day.
To find the work done by 3 builders in one day, we divide the total work by the number of builders:
\[ \text{Work done by 3 builders in 1 day} = \frac{1}{(3 builders * 8 days)} \]

Step 4: Calculate the work rate of 6 builders.
Since 6 builders work at the same rate as 3 builders, the work rate of 6 builders is twice the work rate of 3 builders.

Step 5: Calculate the time it takes for 6 builders to complete the cottage.

\[ \text{Time for 6 builders} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Work rate of 3 builders in 1 day} \]

\[ \text{Time for 6 builders} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{(3 builders * 8 days)} \]
