SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of purple beads.
50 rows × 20 beads per row = 1000 purple beads

Step 2: Calculate the total number of blue beads.
40 rows × 18 beads per row = 720 blue beads

Step 3: Add the number of gold beads.
Gold beads = 80 beads

Step 4: Calculate the total number of beads.
Total beads = 1000 purple + 720 blue + 80 gold = 1800 beads

Step 5: Calculate the cost of the beads.
Cost = Total beads × Cost per 10 beads
Cost = 1800 × (10/10) = 1800 × 1 = $1800
