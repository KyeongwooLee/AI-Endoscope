QUESTION: An office has 90 workers. 2/3rds of them are men and the rest are women. The company hires 10 new employees and 100% of them are women. What is the total percentage of women in the company now?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of men initially in the company.
- Total workers = 90
- Men = 2/3 * 90 = 60

Step 2: Calculate the number of women initially in the company.
- Women = Total workers - Men = 90 - 60 = 30

Step 3: Calculate the new number of workers after hiring 10 new women.
- New total workers = 90 + 10 = 100
- New women = 100 - 30 + 10 = 80

Step 4: Calculate the new total percentage of women in the company.
- New women percentage = (New women / New total workers) * 100 = (80 / 100) * 100 = 80%
