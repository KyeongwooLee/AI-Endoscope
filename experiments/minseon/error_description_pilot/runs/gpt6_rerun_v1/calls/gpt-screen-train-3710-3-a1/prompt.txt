QUESTION: 4 students participated in a 200m race. If the average completion time of the last three students was 35 seconds, and the average completion time of all four runners was 30 seconds, how long (in seconds) did it take the student who came first to finish the race?
SOLUTION: #### 25
To find the time it took the first student to finish the race, we start by using the information given:

1. The average completion time of the last three students is 35 seconds. Since there are 3 students, the total time for these three students is:
   3 * 35 = 105 seconds

2. The average completion time of all four runners is 30 seconds. Therefore, the total time for all four students is:
   4 * 30 = 120 seconds

3. The total time for the last three students is 105 seconds. The time for the first student is the total time minus the time for the last three students:
   120 - 105 = 15 seconds

So, the time it took the student who came first to finish the race is 15 seconds.
