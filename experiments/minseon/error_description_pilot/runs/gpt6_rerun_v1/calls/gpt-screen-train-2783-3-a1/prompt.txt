QUESTION: Jude bought three chairs for his house, all at the same price. He also bought a table that costs $50 and two sets of plates at $20 for each set. After giving the cashier $130, Jude got a $4 change. How much did each of the chairs cost?
SOLUTION: #### 20

Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Total cost of table and plates:**
   - Table costs $50
   - Two sets of plates cost $20 each, so \( 2 \times 20 = $40 \)
   - Total for table and plates: \( 50 + 40 = $90 \)

2. **Amount given to the cashier:**
   - Jude gave the cashier $130

3. **Change received:**
   - Jude received $4 in change

4. **Cost of chairs:**
   - Total cost of table and plates is $90
   - Jude received $4 in change, so the total cost of the chairs and table and plates combined is \( 130 - 4 = $126 \)

5. **Cost of three chairs:**
   - Since Jude bought three chairs at the same price, let's denote the price of one chair as \( x \)
   - Therefore, the cost of three chairs is \( 3x \)

6. **Setting up the equation:**
   - The cost of three chairs plus the table and plates is $126
   - So, \( 3x + 90 = 126 \)

7. **Solving for \( x \):**
   - Subtract 90 from both sides: \( 3x = 126 - 90 \)
   - Simplify: \( 3x = 36 \)
   - Divide both sides by 3: \( x = 12 \)

Thus, the cost of each chair is \( \boxed{20} \) dollars.
