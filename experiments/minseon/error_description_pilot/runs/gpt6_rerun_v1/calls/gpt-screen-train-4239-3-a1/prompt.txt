   \[
   30 \text{ burgers} - 1 \text{ (meatless burger)} - 1 \text{ (breadless burger)} = 28 \text{ burgers}
   \]

4. **Buns Needed**: Each pack of buns contains 8 buns.
   \[
   \text{Number of packs needed} = \frac{28 \text{ burgers}}{8 \text{ buns/pack}} = 3.5 \text{ packs}
