QUESTION: John has to replace the ball bearings for machines he works with.  He has 10 machines and they take 30 ball bearings each.  It normally costs $1 per ball bearing but right now there is a sale where they are only $.75.  Also since he is buying in bulk he gets a further 20% discount.  How much money did he save by buying them all during the sale rather than 1 at a time?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of ball bearings needed.
Total ball bearings = Number of machines * Ball bearings per machine
Total ball bearings = 10 * 30
Total ball bearings = 300

Step 2: Calculate the cost without any discount.
Cost without discount = Total ball bearings * Cost per ball bearing
Cost without discount = 300 * $1
Cost without discount = $300

Step 3: Calculate the cost during the sale.
Cost during sale = Total ball bearings * Sale price per ball bearing
Cost during sale = 300 * $.75
Cost during sale = $225

Step 4: Calculate the discount amount.
Discount amount = Cost without discount - Cost during sale
Discount amount = $300 - $225
Discount amount = $75

Step 5: Calculate the total savings.
Total savings = Cost without discount - Cost during sale
Total savings = $300 - $225
Total savings = $75
