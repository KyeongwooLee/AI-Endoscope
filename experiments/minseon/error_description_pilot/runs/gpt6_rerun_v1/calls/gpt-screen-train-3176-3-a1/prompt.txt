QUESTION: Cynthia has three children whose ages add up to 35. Matthew is two years older than Rebecca and four years younger than Freddy. How many years old is Freddy?
SOLUTION: Let's denote the ages of the children as follows:
- Let \( M \) be Matthew's age,
- Let \( R \) be Rebecca's age,
- Let \( F \) be Freddy's age.

We are given the following information:
1. The total age of the three children is 35:
   \[
   M + R + F = 35
   \]
2. Matthew is two years older than Rebecca:
   \[
   M = R + 2
   \]
3. Matthew is four years younger than Freddy:
   \[
   M = F - 4
   \]

We can use these equations to solve for Freddy's age.

First, substitute \( M = R + 2 \) into the third equation:
\[
R + 2 = F - 4
\]

Next, solve for \( R \):
\[
R + 4 = F
\]
\[
R = F - 4
\]

Now substitute \( R = F - 4 \) into the first equation:
\[
M + (F - 4) + F = 35
\]
\[
M + 2F - 4 = 35
\]
\[
M + 2F = 39
\]

We already know \( M = R + 2 \) and \( R = F - 4 \), so:
\[
M = (F - 4) + 2
\]
\[
M = F - 2
\]

Substitute \( M = F - 2 \) into \( M + 2F = 39 \):
\[
(F - 2) + 2F = 39
\]
\[
3F - 2 = 39
\]
\[
3F = 41
\]
\[
F = \frac{41}{3}
\]
\[
F = 13.67
\]

Thus, Freddy is approximately 13.67 years old.
