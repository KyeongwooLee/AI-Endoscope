QUESTION: After a trip, Mrs. Nalani took the students to the restaurant and told them they could order either a burger or a hot dog. If the number of students who ordered burgers was 30, twice the number of students who called for a hot dog, calculate the number of students at the restaurant.
SOLUTION: #### 90

Let's denote the number of students who ordered hot dogs as \( h \).

According to the problem, the number of students who ordered burgers is 30, and this is twice the number of students who ordered hot dogs. Therefore, we can write the equation:
\[ 30 = 2h \]

To find \( h \), we divide both sides of the equation by 2:
\[ h = \frac{30}{2} \]
\[ h = 15 \]

So, the number of students who ordered hot dogs is 15.

To find the total number of students at the restaurant, we add the number of students who ordered burgers and the number of students who ordered hot dogs:
\[ 30 + 15 = 45 \]

Thus, the total number of students at the restaurant is 45.
