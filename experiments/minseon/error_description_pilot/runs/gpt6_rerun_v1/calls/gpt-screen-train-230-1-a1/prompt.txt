QUESTION: Emery and Serena go to their school library, and each borrows a copy of The life of Jack Steve's book to read for their school project. If Emery can read five times as fast as Serena, and the book takes her 20 days to read, what's the average number of days the two take to read the book?
SOLUTION: #### 15

Reasoning:
1. Let's denote the number of days Serena takes to read the book as \( S \).
2. Since Emery reads five times faster than Serena, Emery's reading time is \( \frac{S}{5} \).
3. We know Emery takes 20 days to read the book, so \( \frac{S}{5} = 20 \).
4. Solving for \( S \), we get \( S = 20 \times 5 = 100 \).
5. The total number of days both take to read the book is \( 20 + 100 = 120 \).
6. The average number of days is \( \frac{120}{2} = 60 \div 2 = 30 \).

Therefore, the average number of days the two take to read the book is 15.
