QUESTION: There are 180 students in ninth grade. 1/4 of them bombed their finals because they were going through difficult breakups. 1/3rd of the rest didn't show up to take the test, and another 20 got less than a D. How many students passed their finals?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of students who bombed their finals.
1/4 of 180 students = 180 * 1/4 = 45 students

Step 2: Calculate the number of students who didn't show up to take the test.
1/3 of the remaining students = (180 - 45) * 1/3 = 135 * 1/3 = 45 students

Step 3: Calculate the number of students who got less than a D.
20 students got less than a D

Step 4: Calculate the number of students who passed their finals.
Total students - students who bombed finals - students who didn't show up - students who got less than a D
180 - 45 - 45 - 20 = 90 students
