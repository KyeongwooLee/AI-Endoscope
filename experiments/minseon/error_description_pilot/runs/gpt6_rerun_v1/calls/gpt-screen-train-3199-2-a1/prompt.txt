QUESTION: Horatio is a poet composing sonnets for his lady fair. He believes it will take many of his sonnets to win her over. Each sonnet is fourteen lines long. His lady fair tells him to leave her alone after he reads her only seven sonnets, and Horatio is heartbroken over the 70 romantic lines he wrote that she never heard. How many sonnets did Horatio write in all?
SOLUTION: Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Total Romantic Lines Written:**
   - Horatio wrote 70 romantic lines.

2. **Romantic Lines Per Sonnet:**
   - Each sonnet is 14 lines long.

3. **Number of Sonnets Written:**
   - To find the total number of sonnets, we divide the total number of romantic lines by the number of lines per sonnet.
   \[
   \text{Number of Sonnets} = \frac{\text{Total Romantic Lines}}{\text{Lines per Sonnet}} = \frac{70}{14}
   \]

4. **Calculation:**
   \[
   \frac{70}{14} = 5
   \]

#### 5
Therefore, Horatio wrote 5 sonnets in all.
