QUESTION: John carves a giant marble rectangular prism 8 meters tall with a 2-meter square base.  It has a density of 2700 kg per cubic meter.  How much does it weigh?
SOLUTION: To find the weight of the marble rectangular prism, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Calculate the volume of the rectangular prism:**
   - The base of the prism is a square with a side length of 2 meters.
   - The height of the prism is 8 meters.
   - The volume \( V \) of a rectangular prism is given by the formula:
     \[
     V = \text{length} \times \text{width} \times \text{height}
     \]
   - Substituting the given values:
     \[
     V = 2 \, \text{m} \times 2 \, \text{m} \times 8 \, \text{m} = 32 \, \text{m}^3
     \]

2. **Calculate the mass of the rectangular prism:**
   - The density \( \rho \) of the marble is 2700 kg per cubic meter.
   - The mass \( m \) of the rectangular prism is given by the formula:
     \[
     m = \rho \times V
     \]
   - Substituting the given values:
     \[
     m = 2700 \, \text{kg/m}^3 \times 32 \, \text{m}^3 = 86400 \, \text{kg}
     \]

3. **Calculate the weight of the rectangular prism:**
   - The weight \( W \) of the rectangular prism is given by the formula:
     \[
     W = m \times g
     \]
   - Assuming the acceleration due to gravity \( g \) is approximately 9.81 m/s²:
     \[
     W = 86400 \, \text{kg} \times 9.81 \, \text{m/s}^2 = 849144 \, \text{N}
     \]
