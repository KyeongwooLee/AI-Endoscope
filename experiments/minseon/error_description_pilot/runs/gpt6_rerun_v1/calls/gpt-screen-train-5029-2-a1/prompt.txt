SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the sweaters.
Cost of one sweater = $30
Number of sweaters = 6
Total cost of sweaters = $30 × 6

Step 2: Calculate the total cost of the scarves.
Cost of one scarf = $20
Number of scarves = 6
Total cost of scarves = $20 × 6

Step 3: Calculate the total cost of all items.
Total cost = Total cost of sweaters + Total cost of scarves

Step 4: Subtract the total cost from Maria's savings.
Maria's savings = $500
Remaining savings = Maria's savings - Total cost

Step 5: Calculate the final amount left in her savings.
Final amount left = Remaining savings
