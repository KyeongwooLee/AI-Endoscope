QUESTION: Jerry takes 2 antacids that weigh 2 grams each and are 5% zinc by weight. Then he takes three more smaller antacids that weigh 1 gram each and have 15% zinc. How many milligrams of zinc does he eat?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total weight of the first 2 antacids.
2 grams each * 2 = 4 grams

Step 2: Calculate the amount of zinc in the first 2 antacids.

3 grams each * 3 = 9 grams

Step 4: Calculate the amount of zinc in the next 3 antacids.
9 grams * 15% = 1.35 grams

Step 5: Calculate the total amount of zinc Jerry eats.
0.2 grams + 1.35 grams = 1.55 grams

Step 6: Convert grams to milligrams.
1.55 grams * 1000 = 1550 milligrams
