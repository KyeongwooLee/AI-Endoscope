QUESTION: Exactly two-fifths of NBA players who signed up for a test are aged between 25 and 35 years. If three-eighths of them are older than 35, and a total of 1000 players signed up, how many players are younger than 25 years?
SOLUTION: #### 200

Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Total number of players signed up**: 1000
2. **Number of players aged between 25 and 35**: \( \frac{2}{5} \times 1000 = 400 \)
3. **Number of players older than 35**: \( \frac{3}{8} \times 1000 = 375 \)
4. **Number of players younger than 25**: We need to subtract the players aged between 25 and 35 and the players older than 35 from the total number of players.

\[
\text{Number of players younger than 25} = 1000 - 400 - 375 = 200
\]

So, the number of players younger than 25 years is 200.
