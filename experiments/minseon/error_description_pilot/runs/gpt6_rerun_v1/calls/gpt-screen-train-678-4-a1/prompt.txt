QUESTION: Belinda can throw a ball at a speed of 20 feet/second. If the ball flies for 8 seconds before hitting the ground, and Belinda's border collie can run 5 feet/second, how many seconds will it take the border collie to catch up to the ball?
SOLUTION: #### 12.0
To determine how long it will take Belinda's border collie to catch up to the ball, we need to calculate the distance the ball travels and then figure out how long it takes for the collie to cover that distance.

1. Calculate the distance the ball travels:
   - The ball's speed is 20 feet/second.
   - The ball flies for 8 seconds.
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 20 feet/second × 8 seconds = 160 feet

2. Determine the time it takes for the border collie to cover the same distance:
   - The border collie's speed is 5 feet/second.
   - Distance = 160 feet.
   - Time = Distance / Speed
   - Time = 160 feet / 5 feet/second = 32 seconds

Therefore, it will take the border collie 32 seconds to catch up to the ball.
