QUESTION: Frank's laundry detergent is double concentrated and will wash 80 loads of laundry.  His detergent is usually $25.00 a bottle but they're having a sale.  If he buys 2 bottles, both bottles will drop to $20.00 a bottle.  How much does he spend, in cents, per load of laundry, if he buys 2 bottles of detergent?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total cost for 2 bottles of detergent at the sale price.
Sale price per bottle = $20.00
Total cost for 2 bottles = 2 × $20.00 = $40.00

Step 2: Calculate the total number of loads that can be washed with the 2 bottles.
Total number of loads = 80 loads

Step 3: Calculate the cost per load of laundry.
Cost per load = Total cost for 2 bottles / Total number of loads
Cost per load = $40.00 / 80 loads = $0.50 per load

Step 4: Convert the cost per load from dollars to cents.
Cost per load in cents = $0.50 × 100 cents/dollar = 50 cents

#### 50
The final answer is 50 cents per load of laundry.
