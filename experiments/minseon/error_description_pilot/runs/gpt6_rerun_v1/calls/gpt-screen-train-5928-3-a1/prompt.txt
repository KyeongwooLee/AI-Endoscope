QUESTION: Three companies, A, B, and C, each purchased 10 ad spaces on a newspaper, with each ad space having a size of 12 foot by 5-foot rectangle. If each square foot ad was charged at $60, how much money did the three pay for the ads combined?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the area of one ad space
Area = length x width
Area = 12 ft x 5 ft = 60 sq ft

Step 2: Calculate the total area for all 10 ad spaces
Total area = Area per ad x Number of ads
Total area = 60 sq ft x 10 = 600 sq ft

Step 3: Calculate the cost per square foot
Cost per sq ft = Price per sq ft
Cost per sq ft = $60

Step 4: Calculate the total cost for all the ads
Total cost = Total area x Cost per sq ft
Total cost = 600 sq ft x $60 = $36,000
