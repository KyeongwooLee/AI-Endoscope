QUESTION: A group of 6 students organized a fundraiser to go to Nicaragua for the summer. For them to go on the trip, each of them needs at least $450. On top of that, they need $3000 for their miscellaneous collective expenses. On the first day of their fundraiser, they receive $600. On the second day, they receive $900, and on the third day, they receive $400. For the next 4 days, they receive only half of what they raised on the first 3 days. How much more money does each person need to raise for them to reach their goal?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total amount of money needed for the trip and miscellaneous expenses.
Total = Miscellaneous expenses + Fundraiser expenses
Total = $3000 + $450 * 6
Total = $3000 + $2700
Total = $5700

Step 2: Calculate the total amount of money raised in the first 3 days.
Money raised in the first 3 days = $600 + $900 + $400
Money raised in the first 3 days = $1900

Step 3: Calculate the amount of money raised in the next 4 days.
Money raised in the next 4 days = 1/2 * $1900
Money raised in the next 4 days = $950

Step 4: Calculate the total amount of money raised after 7 days.
Total raised after 7 days = Money raised in the first 3 days + Money raised in the next 4 days
Total raised after 7 days = $1900 + $950
Total raised after 7 days = $2850

Step 5: Calculate the total amount of money needed to reach the goal.
Total needed = Total raised after 7 days + Miscellaneous expenses
Total needed = $2850 + $3000
Total needed = $5850

Step 6: Calculate the amount of money still needed to reach the goal.
Money still needed = Total needed - Total raised after 7 days
Money still needed = $5850 - $2850
Money still needed = $3000

Step 7: Calculate the amount of money each person needs to raise.
Money each person needs = Money still needed / Number of students
Money each person needs = $3000 / 6
Money each person needs = $500
