QUESTION: Johny traveled South 40 miles, then turned East and traveled for 20 more miles than the distance he took to travel to the south. If he turned North and traveled twice the distance he had traveled to the East, calculate the total distance his journey took.
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the distance traveled to the East.

40 miles + 20 miles = 60 miles

40 miles (South) + 60 miles (East) = 100 miles

2 * 60 miles = 120 miles

Step 4: Calculate the total distance of the journey.
The total distance of the journey is the sum of the distances traveled to the South, East, and North:
100 miles (South) + 60 miles (East) + 120 miles (North) = 280 miles
