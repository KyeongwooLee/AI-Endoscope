1. **Parking Cost**: $10
2. **Entry Fee**: $55
3. **Meal Pass**: $25
4. **Gas Cost**:

   \[
   165 \text{ miles}
   \]

   Next, determine the total fuel needed for the trip:
   \[
   \text{Fuel needed} = \frac{165 \text{ miles}}{30 \text{ miles per gallon}} = 5.5 \text{ gallons}
   \]

   Then, calculate the total gas cost:
   \[
   \text{Gas cost} = 5.5 \text{ gallons} \times 3 \text{ dollars per gallon} = 16.5 \text{ dollars}
   \]

5. **Total Cost of the Trip**:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 10 + 55 + 25 + 16.5 = 106.5 \text{ dollars}
   \]

6. **Amount Sally has already saved**:
   \[
   \text{Amount saved} = 28 \text{ dollars}
   \]

7. **Additional amount Sally needs to save**:
   \[
   \text{Additional savings needed} = 106.5 - 28 = 78.5 \text{ dollars}
