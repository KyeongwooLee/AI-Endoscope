QUESTION: Leila spent $40 which is 1/4 of her money on a sweater. She was then left with $20 after spending the rest of her money on buying pieces of jewelry. How much more did Leila spend on jewelry than on the sweater?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Let's denote the total amount of money Leila had as \( x \).

Step 2: According to the problem, Leila spent \( \frac{1}{4} \) of her money on a sweater. So, the amount spent on the sweater is \( \frac{x}{4} \).

Step 3: We know that the amount spent on the sweater is $40. Therefore, we can write:
   \[
   \frac{x}{4} = 40
   \]

Step 4: Solving for \( x \):
   \[
   x = 40 \times 4 = 160
   \]

Step 5: Leila spent the remaining money on jewelry. She was left with $20 after buying jewelry. Therefore, the amount spent on jewelry is:
   \[
   160 - 20 = 140
   \]

Step 6: To find out how much more Leila spent on jewelry than on the sweater:
   \[
   140 - 40 = 100
   \]
