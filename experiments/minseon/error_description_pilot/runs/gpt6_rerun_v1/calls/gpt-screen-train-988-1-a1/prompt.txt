QUESTION: Justin has a box that is 12 inches in height. The length of the box is 3 times its height and 4 times its width. What is the volume of the box?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Identify the dimensions of the box.
- Height (h) = 12 inches
- Length (l) = 3 times height = 3 * 12 = 36 inches
- Width (w) = Length / 4 = 36 / 4 = 9 inches

Step 2: Calculate the volume of the box.
Volume (V) = Length * Width * Height
V = 36 * 9 * 12
V = 432 * 12
V = 5184 cubic inches
