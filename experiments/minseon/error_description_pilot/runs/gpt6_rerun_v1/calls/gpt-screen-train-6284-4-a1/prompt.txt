QUESTION: Lola and Tara decide to race to the top of a 20 story building. Tara takes the elevator and Lola runs up the stairs. Lola can run up 1 story in 10 seconds. The elevator goes up a story in 8 seconds but stops for 3 seconds on every single floor. How long would it take for the slower one of Lola and Tara to reach the top floor?
SOLUTION: To solve this problem, let's break down the time taken by each person:

1. **Tara's time:**
   - Tara takes the elevator.
   - The elevator goes up one story in 8 seconds.
   - There are 20 stories in total, so the elevator takes \(20 \times 8 = 160\) seconds to reach the top floor.
   - The elevator stops for 3 seconds on every floor, but since it stops on the first floor, the total time for Tara is 163 seconds (160 seconds for going up + 3 seconds for the stop).

2. **Lola's time:**
   - Lola runs up one story in 10 seconds.
   - Lola has to run 20 stories, so the time taken by Lola is \(20 \times 10 = 200\) seconds.

Now, comparing the times:
- Tara's total time: 163 seconds
- Lola's total time: 200 seconds

Since Lola runs the stairs and Tara takes the elevator, the slower one is Lola.

#### 200
This is the total time it would take for Lola to reach the top floor.
