- Each box requires one piece of tape as long as the long side (30 inches).
- Each box requires two pieces of tape as long as the short side (15 inches).
- Tape needed for one box = 30 + 2 * 15 = 70 inches.

Step 2: Calculate the tape needed for the 5 boxes of 15 inches by 30 inches.
- Tape needed for 5 boxes = 5 * 70 = 350 inches.

Step 3: Calculate the tape needed for one box of 40 inches square.
- Each box requires one piece of tape as long as the long side (40 inches).
- Each box requires two pieces of tape as long as the short side (40 inches).
- Tape needed for one box = 40 + 2 * 40 = 120 inches.
