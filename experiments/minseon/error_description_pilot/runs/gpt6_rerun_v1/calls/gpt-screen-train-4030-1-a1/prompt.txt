QUESTION: In the beginning, Justine had 10 more rubber bands than Bailey but 2 fewer bands than Ylona. Bailey decided to give two bands each to Justine and Ylona so that he is left with only 8 rubber bands. How many rubber bands did Ylona have in the beginning?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Let's denote the number of rubber bands Ylona had in the beginning as \( y \).

Step 2: According to the problem, Bailey had \( y - 2 \) rubber bands in the beginning.
Step 3: Justine had \( y - 2 + 10 = y + 8 \) rubber bands in the beginning.

Step 4: Bailey gave 2 bands to Justine and 2 bands to Ylona. After giving away the bands, Bailey is left with 8 bands. So, the total number of bands Bailey had initially minus the bands he gave away equals 8:
\[ (y - 2) - 2 + 2 = 8 \]

Step 5: Simplify the equation:
\[ y - 2 = 8 \]

Step 6: Solve for \( y \):
\[ y = 8 + 2 \]
\[ y = 10 \]
