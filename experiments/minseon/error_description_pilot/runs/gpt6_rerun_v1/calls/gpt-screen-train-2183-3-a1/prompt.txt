QUESTION: Andrew installed hardwood flooring in his house. His bedroom took eight wooden planks, his living room took twenty planks, and his kitchen took eleven planks. The guest bedroom took two fewer planks than Andrew’s bedroom, and each of his two hallways took four planks. He accidentally ruined three planks in each bedroom by dragging furniture over them and had to replace them. He ended up with six leftover planks at the end. How many wooden planks did Andrew buy to start with?

1. **Bedroom Planks**:
   - Andrew's bedroom took 8 planks.
   - Guest bedroom took 2 fewer planks than Andrew’s bedroom.
   - Hallways each took 4 planks, and there are two hallways.

   Total planks for bedrooms and hallways:
   \[
   8 + (8 - 2) + 2 \times 4 = 8 + 6 + 8 = 22
   \]

   - Living room took 20 planks.

3. **Kitchen Planks**:
   - Kitchen took 11 planks.

4. **Repaired Planks**:
   - Andrew ruined 3 planks in each bedroom.

   Total planks ruined:
   \[
   3 \times 3 = 9
   \]

5. **Leftover Planks**:
   - Andrew ended up with 6 leftover planks.

6. **Initial Planks Calculation**:
   - We need to find the initial number of planks Andrew bought.

   Total planks used initially:
   \[
   22 + 20 + 11 = 53
   \]

   \[
   53 + 6 = 59
   \]
