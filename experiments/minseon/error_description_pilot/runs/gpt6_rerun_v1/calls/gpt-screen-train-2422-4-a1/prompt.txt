QUESTION: Hershel has 10 betta fish and 15 goldfish in a bowl. His friend Bexley brings him 2/5 times as many betta fish and 1/3 times as many goldfish. If Hershel gifts his sister 1/2 of the fish, calculate the total number of fish he has remaining in the bowl.
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of betta fish Bexley brings.
Bexley brings 2/5 times as many betta fish as Hershel originally has.
Bexley brings 2/5 * 10 = 4 betta fish.

Step 2: Calculate the number of goldfish Bexley brings.
Bexley brings 1/3 times as many goldfish as Hershel originally has.
Bexley brings 1/3 * 15 = 5 goldfish.

Step 3: Calculate the total number of fish before gifting.
Total fish before gifting = betta fish + goldfish
Total fish before gifting = 10 + 15 = 25 fish

Step 4: Calculate the number of fish given to Hershel's sister.
Hershel gives 1/2 of the fish to his sister.
Fish given to sister = 1/2 * 25 = 12.5 fish (since we can't have half a fish, we'll consider 12 or 13, but for simplicity in this calculation, we'll assume he gives 12 fish)

Step 5: Calculate the total number of fish remaining.
Total fish remaining = total fish before gifting - fish given to sister
Total fish remaining = 25 - 12 = 13 fish

#### 13
Hershel has 13 fish remaining in the bowl.
