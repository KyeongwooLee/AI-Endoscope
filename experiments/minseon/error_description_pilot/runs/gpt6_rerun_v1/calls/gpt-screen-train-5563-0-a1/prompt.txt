QUESTION: A park warden has issued 24 citations over the past three hours. He issued the same number for littering as he did for off-leash dogs, and he issued double the number of other citations for parking fines. How many littering citations did the warden issue?
SOLUTION: Let's denote the number of littering citations as \( L \), the number of off-leash dog citations as \( D \), and the number of parking fine citations as \( P \).

From the problem, we know:
1. The total number of citations is 24:
   \[
   L + D + P = 24
   \]
2. The number of littering citations is the same as the number of off-leash dog citations:
   \[
   L = D
   \]
3. The number of parking fine citations is double the number of littering citations:
   \[
   P = 2L
   \]

Substitute \( D \) and \( P \) in terms of \( L \) into the total citations equation:
\[
L + L + 2L = 24
\]
Combine like terms:
\[
4L = 24
\]
Solve for \( L \):
\[
L = \frac{24}{4} = 6
\]

Therefore, the warden issued 6 littering citations.

#### 6
The final answer is 6.
