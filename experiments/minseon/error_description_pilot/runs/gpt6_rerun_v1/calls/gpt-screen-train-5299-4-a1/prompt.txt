37% of 1000 customers return their books.
\[ 37\% \text{ of } 1000 = \frac{37}{100} \times 1000 = 370 \text{ customers} \]

\[ \text{Total revenue from returns} = 370 \times 15 = 5550 \text{ dollars} \]
