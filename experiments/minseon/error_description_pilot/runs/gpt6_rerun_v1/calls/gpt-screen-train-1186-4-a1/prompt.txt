QUESTION: John decides to install a ramp in his house.  He needs to get permits which cost $250.  After that, he hires a contractor which costs $150 an hour and the guy works for 3 days at 5 hours per day.  He also pays an inspector 80% less to make sure it is OK.  How much was the total cost?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the cost of hiring the contractor.
The cost is $150 per hour and the contractor works for 3 days at 5 hours per day.
\[ \text{Cost of contractor} = 150 \times 3 \times 5 = 2250 \]

Step 2: Calculate the cost of the inspector.
The inspector is paid 80% less than the regular rate, which is $150 per hour.
\[ \text{Regular rate} = 150 \]
\[ \text{Discount} = 150 \times 0.8 = 120 \]
\[ \text{Cost of inspector} = 150 - 120 = 30 \]

Step 3: Calculate the total cost by adding the cost of permits, contractor, and inspector.
\[ \text{Total cost} = 250 + 2250 + 30 = 2530 \]

#### 2530
This is the total cost.
