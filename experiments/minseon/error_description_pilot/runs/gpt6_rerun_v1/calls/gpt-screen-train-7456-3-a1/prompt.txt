QUESTION: Mary went to the store to buy fruit. Apples cost $1, oranges cost $2, and bananas cost $3. For every 5 fruits that customers buy, the store offers a $1 discount. Mary buys 5 apples, 3 oranges, and 2 bananas. How much will she pay?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total cost of each type of fruit.
Cost of 5 apples = 5 * $1 = $5
Cost of 3 oranges = 3 * $2 = $6
Cost of 2 bananas = 2 * $3 = $6

Step 2: Sum the costs of all fruits.
Total cost = $5 + $6 + $6 = $17

Step 3: Calculate the discount.
Number of sets of 5 fruits = 5 / 5 = 1
Discount per set = $1
Total discount = 1 * $1 = $1

Step 4: Subtract the total discount from the total cost.
Final amount to pay = $17 - $1 = $16

The final answer is $16.
