QUESTION: One of the clubs at Georgia's school was selling carnations for a fundraising event. A carnation costs $0.50.  They also had a special where you could buy one dozen carnations for $4.00.  If Georgia sent a dozen carnations to each of her 5 teachers and bought one carnation for each of her 14 friends, how much money would she spend?
SOLUTION: To calculate the total cost, we need to consider both the individual carnations and the dozen carnations for teachers.

1. **Cost of individual carnations for friends:**
   - Georgia has 14 friends.
   - Each carnation costs $0.50.
   - Total cost for friends = 14 * $0.50 = $7.00

2. **Cost of dozen carnations for teachers:**
   - Georgia has 5 teachers.
   - Each dozen carnations costs $4.00.
   - Total cost for teachers = 5 * $4.00 = $20.00

3. **Total cost:**
   - Total cost = Cost for friends + Cost for teachers
   - Total cost = $7.00 + $20.00 = $27.00

#### 27
The total amount of money Georgia would spend is $27.00.
