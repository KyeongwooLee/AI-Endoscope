QUESTION: Zion made a triangular-shaped house of cards with the base having a measurement of 40 centimeters and a height of 20 centimeters. If two of his friends also built similar shaped houses, what's the total area of the triangular houses that Zion and his friends made (the area of a triangle is 1/2 * width * height)?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the area of Zion's triangular house of cards.
Area of Zion's house = 1/2 * base * height
Area of Zion's house = 1/2 * 40 cm * 20 cm

Step 2: Since two of Zion's friends built similar shaped houses, we need to double the area of Zion's house.
Total area of houses made by Zion and his friends = 2 * Area of Zion's house

Step 3: Calculate the total area.
Area of Zion's house = 1/2 * 40 cm * 20 cm = 400 cm²
Total area of houses made by Zion and his friends = 2 * 400 cm² = 800 cm²

#### 800
The total area of the triangular houses that Zion and his friends made is 800 cm².
