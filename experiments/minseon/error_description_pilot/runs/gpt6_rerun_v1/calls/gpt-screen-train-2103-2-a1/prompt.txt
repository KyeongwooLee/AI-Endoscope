SOLUTION: To determine how many pounds of sand are needed to fill the sandbox, we first need to calculate the total surface area of the sandbox that needs to be filled with sand.

1. **Calculate the area of one side of the sandbox:**
   \[
   \text{Area of one side} = \text{side length} \times \text{side length} = 40 \, \text{inches} \times 40 \, \text{inches} = 1600 \, \text{square inches}
   \]

2. **Calculate the total surface area of the sandbox:**
   \[
   \text{Total surface area} = \text{Area of one side} \times \text{number of sides} = 1600 \, \text{square inches} \times 4 = 6400 \, \text{square inches}
   \]

3. **Determine the amount of sand needed per square inch:**
   \[
   \text{Sand per square inch} = \frac{30 \, \text{pounds}}{80 \, \text{square inches}} = 0.375 \, \text{pounds per square inch}
   \]

4. **Calculate the total amount of sand needed to fill the sandbox:**
   \[
   \text{Total sand needed} = \text{Total surface area} \times \text{Sand per square inch} = 6400 \, \text{square inches} \times 0.375 \, \text{pounds per square inch} = 2400 \, \text{pounds}
