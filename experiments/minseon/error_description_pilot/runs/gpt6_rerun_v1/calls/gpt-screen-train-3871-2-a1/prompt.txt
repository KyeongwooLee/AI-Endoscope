QUESTION: A class is completing an art project. Each of the 30 students is assigned to a separate group and will need to take markers from the 22 boxes of markers. The first group has 10 students who will have 2 markers each and the second group has 15 students who will have 4 markers each. The last group takes the remaining markers and divides them equally. If each box of markers contains 5 markers, how many markers does each of the students in the last group receive?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of markers taken by the first group.
First group has 10 students and each has 2 markers.
\( 10 \times 2 = 20 \) markers

Step 2: Calculate the total number of markers taken by the second group.
Second group has 15 students and each has 4 markers.
\( 15 \times 4 = 60 \) markers

Step 3: Calculate the total number of markers taken by the first and second groups.
\( 20 + 60 = 80 \) markers

Step 4: Calculate the total number of markers in the 22 boxes.
\( 22 \times 5 = 110 \) markers

Step 5: Calculate the number of markers remaining for the last group.
\( 110 - 80 = 30 \) markers

Step 6: Calculate the number of students in the last group.
\( 30 \) students

Step 7: Calculate the number of markers each student in the last group receives.
\( 30 \div 30 = 1 \) marker

#### 1
Each of the students in the last group receives 1 marker.
