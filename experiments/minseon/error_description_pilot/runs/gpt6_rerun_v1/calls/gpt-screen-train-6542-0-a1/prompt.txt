QUESTION: Bill is a painter who is hired to paint a client’s house. The house has three bedrooms, and twice as many other rooms as bedrooms. The client wants each bedroom painted a different color, and the other rooms painted white. Each room takes 2 gallons of paint. Color paint comes in 1-gallon paint cans, and white paint comes in 3-gallon cans. How many cans of paint will Bill need for the whole house?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the number of other rooms.
The house has three bedrooms and twice as many other rooms as bedrooms.
Number of other rooms = 2 * Number of bedrooms = 2 * 3 = 6

Step 2: Calculate the total number of rooms.
Total number of rooms = Number of bedrooms + Number of other rooms = 3 + 6 = 9

Step 3: Calculate the total amount of paint needed for the bedrooms.
Each bedroom takes 2 gallons of paint.
Total paint for bedrooms = Number of bedrooms * Paint per bedroom = 3 * 2 = 6 gallons

Step 4: Calculate the total amount of paint needed for the other rooms.
Each other room takes 2 gallons of paint.
Total paint for other rooms = Number of other rooms * Paint per room = 6 * 2 = 12 gallons

Step 5: Calculate the total amount of paint needed for the whole house.
Total paint needed = Paint for bedrooms + Paint for other rooms = 6 + 12 = 18 gallons

Step 6: Calculate the number of cans of paint needed.
Color paint comes in 1-gallon cans.
White paint comes in 3-gallon cans.
Total cans needed = Total paint needed / Paint per can = 18 / 1 = 18 cans

#### 18
Bill will need 18 cans of paint for the whole house.
