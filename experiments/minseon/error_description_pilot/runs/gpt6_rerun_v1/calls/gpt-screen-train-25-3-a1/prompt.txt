QUESTION: Ralph is going to practice playing tennis with a tennis ball machine that shoots out tennis balls for Ralph to hit. He loads up the machine with 175 tennis balls to start with. Out of the first 100 balls, he manages to hit 2/5 of them. Of the next 75 tennis balls, he manages to hit 1/3 of them. Out of all the tennis balls, how many did Ralph not hit?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of balls Ralph hit out of the first 100 balls.
\[ \text{Balls hit} = 2/5 \times 100 \]

Step 2: Calculate the number of balls Ralph hit out of the next 75 balls.
\[ \text{Balls hit} = 1/3 \times 75 \]

Step 3: Calculate the total number of balls Ralph hit.
\[ \text{Total balls hit} = \text{Balls hit out of first 100} + \text{Balls hit out of next 75} \]

Step 4: Calculate the total number of balls Ralph did not hit.
\[ \text{Total balls not hit} = 175 - \text{Total balls hit} \]

#### 60
Ralph did not hit 60 balls.
