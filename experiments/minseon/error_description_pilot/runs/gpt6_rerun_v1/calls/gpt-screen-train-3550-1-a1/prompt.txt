QUESTION: Porter earns $8 per day and works 5 times a week. His manager is asking him to work an extra day that promises him an extra fifty percent on top of his daily rate. How much money will he earn after a month if he renders overtime every week?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate Porter's regular weekly earnings.
Porter earns $8 per day and works 5 days a week.
Regular weekly earnings = $8 * 5 = $40

Step 2: Calculate the extra earnings for the extra day.
Extra day's earnings = 50% of $8 = 0.50 * $8 = $4
Step 3: Calculate the total weekly earnings including the extra day.
Total weekly earnings = Regular weekly earnings + Extra day's earnings = $40 + $4 = $44

Step 4: Calculate the total monthly earnings.
Assuming a month has 4 weeks, total monthly earnings = Total weekly earnings * 4 = $44 * 4 = $176
