QUESTION: Porter is a painter who creates beautiful paintings of wild animals.  Most recently, he painted a mural of a pack of wolves standing on a snowy-white mountainside underneath a full moon.  He put the painting up for auction and it sold for $1000 less than five times more than he had made on his previous painting.  If he received $44,000 for the sale of his most recent painting, how much, in dollars, did he make for selling his previous painting?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Let's denote the amount Porter made from his previous painting as x dollars.

Step 2: According to the problem, the most recent painting sold for $1000 less than five times more than he had made on his previous painting. Therefore, the sale price of the most recent painting can be expressed as 5x + 1000 dollars.

Step 3: We are given that he received $44,000 for the sale of his most recent painting. Thus, we can set up the equation:
\[ 5x + 1000 = 44000 \]

Step 4: Solving for x:
\[ 5x = 44000 - 1000 \]
\[ 5x = 43000 \]
\[ x = \frac{43000}{5} \]
\[ x = 8600 \]

#### 8600
Porter made $8600 for selling his previous painting.
