QUESTION: Robe's car broke and he used $10 from his savings to pay for the repair. Before the repair, he bought 2 kinds of spare parts. A corner light that costs twice the price of the repair fee, and two brake disks; each disk cost thrice the price of the corner light. After that, he had $480 savings left. How much money did Robe have saved before his car broke?
SOLUTION: Let's denote the repair fee by \( x \). According to the problem, the corner light costs \( 2x \) and each brake disk costs \( 3 \times 2x = 6x \). Robe initially had $10 for the repair and then spent all his remaining money after buying the parts.

Let's denote Robe's initial savings by \( y \).

First, we calculate the total cost of the spare parts:
- Cost of the corner light: \( 2x \)
- Cost of the brake disks: \( 2 \times 6x = 12x \)

The total cost of the spare parts is \( 2x + 12x = 14x \).

Robe used $10 for the repair and then spent all his remaining money after buying the parts. After buying the parts, he had $480 left. Therefore, we can write the equation:
\[ y - 10 - 14x = 480 \]

Since \( x \) is the cost of the repair, and \( y \) is Robe's initial savings, we need to find the value of \( y \). We know that Robe spent the entire amount he had initially after buying the parts, so we can simplify the equation to:
\[ y - 14x = 500 \]

From the problem, we know \( x = 10 \). Substituting \( x \) into the equation:
\[ y - 14 \times 10 = 500 \]
\[ y - 140 = 500 \]
\[ y = 640 \]

Therefore, Robe had saved $640 before his car broke.
