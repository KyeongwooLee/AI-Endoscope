QUESTION: Janina spends $30 each day for rent and uses $12 worth of supplies daily to run her pancake stand. If she sells each pancake for $2, how many pancakes must Janina sell each day to cover her expenses?
SOLUTION: #### 15
To determine how many pancakes Janina must sell each day to cover her expenses, we start by calculating her total daily expenses.

1. Janina's daily rent: $30
2. Janina's daily supplies: $12
3. Total daily expenses: $30 + $12 = $42

Janina sells each pancake for $2. To find out how many pancakes she needs to sell to cover her total expenses, we divide the total expenses by the price per pancake:

\[ \text{Number of pancakes} = \frac{\text{Total expenses}}{\text{Price per pancake}} \]
\[ \text{Number of pancakes} = \frac{42}{2} = 21 \]

Therefore, Janina must sell **21** pancakes each day to cover her expenses.
