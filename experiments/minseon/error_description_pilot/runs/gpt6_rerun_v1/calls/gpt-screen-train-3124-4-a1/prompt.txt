   - Nicole starts with 10 pieces of clothing.

2. **Clothing for Nicole's first older sister:**

   - \( 10 + 2 = 12 \) pieces of clothing.

4. **Clothing for Nicole's third older sister:**
   - The third sister has the average of what her three younger sisters have.
   - First sister: 5 pieces
   - Second sister: 12 pieces
   - Average = \( \frac{5 + 12}{2} = \frac{17}{2} = 8.5 \) pieces of clothing.

5. **Total clothing given to Nicole:**
   - Add all the clothing given to Nicole:
   - Nicole's initial clothing: 10
   - First sister's clothing: 5
   - Second sister's clothing: 12
   - Third sister's clothing: 8.5
