QUESTION: Carl has been selling watermelons on the side of the road for $3 each. This evening he went home with $105 in profit and 18 watermelons. How many watermelons did he start out with this morning?
SOLUTION: To find out how many watermelons Carl started with, we need to consider the following:
1. Carl earns $3 per watermelon.
2. He made a profit of $105.
3. He sold 18 watermelons.

First, we calculate the total amount of money Carl earned from selling the watermelons:
\[ \text{Total earnings} = \text{Number of watermelons sold} \times \text{Price per watermelon} \]
\[ \text{Total earnings} = 18 \times 3 = 54 \]

Since Carl made a profit of $105 and earned $54 from selling the watermelons, we can find out how many watermelons he started with by adding the profit to the earnings:
\[ \text{Initial number of watermelons} = \text{Total earnings} + \text{Profit} \]
\[ \text{Initial number of watermelons} = 54 + 105 = 159 \]

Therefore, the answer is:
#### 159
