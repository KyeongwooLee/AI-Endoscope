QUESTION: A truck driver has to spend $2 per gallon of gas. She can drive 10 miles per gallon. She drives at a rate of 30 miles per hour. If she is paid $.50 per mile, how much money does she make if she drives for 10 hours?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total distance driven.
Distance = Speed × Time
Distance = 30 miles per hour × 10 hours = 300 miles

Step 2: Calculate the total money earned from driving.
Money earned = Distance × Rate per mile
Money earned = 300 miles × $0.50 per mile = $150

Step 3: Calculate the total money spent on gas.
Money spent = Distance × Cost per gallon
Money spent = 300 miles × $2 per gallon = $600

Step 4: Calculate the net money made.
Net money = Money earned - Money spent
Net money = $150 - $600 = -$450
