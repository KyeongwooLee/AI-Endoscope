QUESTION: Mr. Wells has a garden of flowers with 50 rows. If each row has 400 flowers and Mr. Wells cuts 60% of the flowers, how many flowers are remaining in the garden?
SOLUTION: #### 100
Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Calculate the total number of flowers initially:**
   - Mr. Wells has 50 rows.
   - Each row has 400 flowers.
   - Therefore, the total number of flowers is:
     \[
     50 \times 400 = 20000
     \]

2. **Determine how many flowers are cut:**
   - Mr. Wells cuts 60% of the flowers.
   - To find 60% of 20000:
     \[
     0.60 \times 20000 = 12000
     \]

3. **Calculate the remaining flowers:**
   - Subtract the number of flowers cut from the total number of flowers:
     \[
     20000 - 12000 = 8000
     \]

So, the number of flowers remaining in the garden is 8000.
