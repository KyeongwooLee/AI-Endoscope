QUESTION: Harry is joining a marathon that's why he practices and varies his workouts from day to day. He ran 10 meters per hour on Monday. From Tuesday to Thursday he ran 50% faster than on Monday. Then on Friday, he ran 60% faster than he ran on Thursday. How fast did Harry run on Friday?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the speed from Tuesday to Thursday.
Speed on Tuesday = Speed on Monday = 10 meters per hour
Speed on Wednesday = Speed on Tuesday + 50% of Speed on Tuesday = 10 + 0.5 * 10 = 15 meters per hour
Speed on Thursday = Speed on Wednesday + 50% of Speed on Wednesday = 15 + 0.5 * 15 = 22.5 meters per hour

Step 2: Calculate the speed on Friday.
Speed on Friday = Speed on Thursday + 60% of Speed on Thursday = 22.5 + 0.6 * 22.5 = 35.25 meters per hour
