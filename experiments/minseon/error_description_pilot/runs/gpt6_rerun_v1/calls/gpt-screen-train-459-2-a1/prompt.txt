QUESTION: A year ago, the total cost of buying a lawnmower was 2/5 times less than the cost it goes for now. If the cost was $1800 a year ago, calculate how much it would cost Mr. Lucian to buy 4 such lawnmowers.
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the current cost of the lawnmower.
- Let the current cost be \( C \).
- According to the problem, 2/5 times less than the current cost was $1800 a year ago.
- This means the cost a year ago was \( C - \frac{2}{5}C = \frac{3}{5}C \).
- Given that \( \frac{3}{5}C = 1800 \), we can find \( C \) by solving for \( C \).

Step 2: Solve for the current cost.
- \( \frac{3}{5}C = 1800 \)
- \( C = 1800 \times \frac{5}{3} \)
- \( C = 3000 \)

Step 3: Calculate the cost of 4 lawnmowers.
- The cost of 4 lawnmowers is \( 4 \times C \).
- \( 4 \times 3000 = 12000 \)

#### 12000
The cost for Mr. Lucian to buy 4 lawnmowers would be $12000.
