QUESTION: Tom weighs 150 kg.  He manages to hold 1.5 times his weight in each hand while wearing a weight vest weighing half his weight.  How much total weight was he moving with?
SOLUTION: Tom's weight is 150 kg.
He holds 1.5 times his weight in each hand, which is \( 1.5 \times 150 = 225 \) kg.

He wears a vest weighing half his weight, which is \( \frac{150}{2} = 75 \) kg.

Total weight moving is the sum of the weight in his hands and the weight of the vest: \( 225 + 75 = 300 \) kg.

#### 300
The total weight Tom was moving with is 300 kg.
