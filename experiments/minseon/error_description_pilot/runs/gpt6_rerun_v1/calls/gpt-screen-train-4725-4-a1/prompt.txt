QUESTION: In Canada, for every moose there are two beavers, and for every beaver there are 19 humans.  If there are 38 million people in Canada, what is the moose population of Canada, in millions?
SOLUTION: To solve this problem, we'll use the given ratios and the total population of humans.

1. **Determine the number of beavers per human:**
   - For every beaver, there are 19 humans.

2. **Determine the number of moose per beaver:**
   - For every moose, there are two beavers.

3. **Calculate the number of humans per beaver:**
   \[
   \text{Number of humans per beaver} = \frac{\text{Number of humans}}{\text{Number of beavers}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of humans per beaver} = \frac{38}{2} = 19
   \]

4. **Calculate the number of beavers per human:**
   \[
   \text{Number of beavers per human} = \frac{\text{Number of beavers}}{\text{Number of humans}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of beavers per human} = \frac{2}{19}
   \]

5. **Calculate the number of moose per human:**
   \[
   \text{Number of moose per human} = \frac{\text{Number of moose}}{\text{Number of beavers per human}}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Number of moose per human} = \frac{1}{\frac{2}{19}} = \frac{19}{2} = 9.5
   \]

6. **Calculate the total moose population:**
   \[
   \text{Total moose population} = \text{Number of moose per human} \times \text{Number of humans}
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total moose population} = 9.5 \times 38 = 361
   \]
