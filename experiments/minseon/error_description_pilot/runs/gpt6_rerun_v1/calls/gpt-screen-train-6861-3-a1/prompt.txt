QUESTION: Ronald and his friend Max want to buy a new video game that was just released. The game costs $60. To earn money, they started selling ice cream in their yard, and they sell each ice cream for $5. How many ice creams will they need to sell for both to be able to afford to buy the game?
SOLUTION: To determine how many ice creams Ronald and Max need to sell, we first need to find out how much money they need together to buy the game.

The cost of the video game is $60. So, they need to earn $60 in total.

Each ice cream is sold for $5. Let's calculate how many ice creams they need to sell to earn $60.

\[
\text{Number of ice creams} = \frac{\text{Total cost of the game}}{\text{Price per ice cream}} = \frac{60}{5}
\]

Now, let's do the division:

\[
\frac{60}{5} = 12
\]

So, Ronald and Max will need to sell 12 ice creams for both of them to be able to afford to buy the game.

#### 12
The answer is 12 ice creams.
