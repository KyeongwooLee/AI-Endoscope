QUESTION: Billy ate 20 apples this week. On Monday, he ate 2 apples. On Tuesday, he ate twice as many as he ate the day before. He’s not sure what he ate on Wednesday. On Thursday, he ate four times as many as he ate on Friday. On Friday, he ate half of the amount he ate on Monday.  How many apples did he eat on Wednesday?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the number of apples Billy ate on Tuesday.
Billy ate 2 apples on Monday and twice as many on Tuesday.
2 * 2 = 4 apples on Tuesday.

Step 2: Determine the number of apples Billy ate on Friday.
Billy ate half of the amount he ate on Monday.
2 / 2 = 1 apple on Friday.

Step 3: Determine the number of apples Billy ate on Thursday.
Billy ate four times as many apples on Thursday as he did on Friday.
4 * 1 = 4 apples on Thursday.

Step 4: Determine the number of apples Billy ate on Wednesday.
We know the total number of apples Billy ate this week is 20.
We also know the number of apples he ate on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday.
20 - (2 + 4 + 1 + 4) = 11 apples on Wednesday.

The answer is 11.
