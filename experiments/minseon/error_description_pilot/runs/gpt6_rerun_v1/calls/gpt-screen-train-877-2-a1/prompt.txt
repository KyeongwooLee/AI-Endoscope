QUESTION: Kimberly went strawberry picking with her family over the weekend. She picked 8 times the amount of strawberries her brother picked and her parents picked 93 strawberries less than her. If her brother picked 3 baskets each containing 15 strawberries, how many strawberries would they each have if they divide the total number of strawberries equally amongst them?
SOLUTION: Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Brother's Strawberries:**
   - Brother picked 3 baskets each containing 15 strawberries.
   - Number of strawberries the brother picked = 3 * 15 = 45 strawberries.

2. **Kimberly's Strawberries:**
   - Kimberly picked 8 times the amount of strawberries her brother picked.
   - Number of strawberries Kimberly picked = 8 * 45 = 360 strawberries.

3. **Parents' Strawberries:**
   - Parents picked 93 strawberries less than Kimberly.
   - Number of strawberries the parents picked = 360 - 93 = 267 strawberries.

4. **Total Strawberries:**
   - Total strawberries = Brother's strawberries + Kimberly's strawberries + Parents' strawberries.
   - Total strawberries = 45 + 360 + 267 = 672 strawberries.

5. **Equal Division:**
   - Total number of people = Kimberly + Brother + Parents = 3 + 1 + 1 = 5 people.
   - Number of strawberries each person would have = Total strawberries / Number of people.
   - Number of strawberries each person would have = 672 / 5 = 134.4 strawberries.

#### 134.4
