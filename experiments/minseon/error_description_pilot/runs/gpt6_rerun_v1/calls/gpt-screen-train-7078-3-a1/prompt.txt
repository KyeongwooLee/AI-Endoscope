QUESTION: There are 3 ducks in a pond and they all eat some pieces of bread thrown in the water. One duck eats half of all the pieces of bread. Another duck eats only 13 pieces of the bread and the last duck eats 7 pieces of bread. There are 30 pieces of bread left in the water, how many total pieces of bread were thrown in the pond?
SOLUTION: Let's denote the total number of pieces of bread thrown in the pond as T.

- One duck eats half of all the pieces of bread, so it eats T/2 pieces.
- Another duck eats 13 pieces.
- The last duck eats 7 pieces.
- There are 30 pieces of bread left in the water.

The sum of the pieces of bread eaten by all the ducks plus the pieces left should equal the total number of pieces thrown in the pond. Therefore, we can write the equation:

\[ \frac{T}{2} + 13 + 7 + 30 = T \]

Now, let's solve for T:

\[ \frac{T}{2} + 40 = T \]

Subtract \(\frac{T}{2}\) from both sides:

\[ 40 = T - \frac{T}{2} \]

\[ 40 = \frac{T}{2} \]

Multiply both sides by 2:

\[ 80 = T \]

#### 80
There were 80 pieces of bread thrown in the pond.
