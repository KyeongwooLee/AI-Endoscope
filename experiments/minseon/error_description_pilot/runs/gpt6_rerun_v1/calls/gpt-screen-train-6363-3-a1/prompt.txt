How high must this player be able to jump to reach 6 inches above the rim to dunk a basketball?
SOLUTION: To determine how high the player must jump to reach 6 inches above the rim, we need to follow these steps:

1. Convert all measurements to the same unit. Let's use inches.
   - The rim is 10 feet above the ground.
   - 6 inches above the rim.
   - 6 feet tall.
   - 22 inches above their head.

   - Total vertical distance = rim height - reach above head
   - Total vertical distance = 10 feet - 6 inches
   - 10 feet = 120 inches
   - 6 inches = 6 inches
   - Total vertical distance = 120 inches - 6 inches = 114 inches

3. Determine the height the player must jump to reach the total vertical distance.
   - Player's height = 6 feet = 72 inches
   - Reach above head = 22 inches
   - Jump height needed = total vertical distance - player's height - reach above head
   - Jump height needed = 114 inches - 72 inches - 22 inches = 20 inches
