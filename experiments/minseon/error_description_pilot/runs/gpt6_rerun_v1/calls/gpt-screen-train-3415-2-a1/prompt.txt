QUESTION: Jose owns a swimming pool. He charges $3 for kids and twice that amount for adults. If 8 kids and 10 adults swim in his swimming pool per day, how much money will he earn per week?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the daily earnings from kids.

\[ 3 \times 8 = 24 \]

Step 2: Calculate the daily earnings from adults.

\[ 2 \times 3 = 6 \]

Step 3: Calculate the total daily earnings.
The total daily earnings from both kids and adults are:
\[ 24 + 6 = 30 \]

Step 4: Calculate the weekly earnings.
There are 7 days in a week, so the weekly earnings are:
\[ 30 \times 7 = 210 \]
