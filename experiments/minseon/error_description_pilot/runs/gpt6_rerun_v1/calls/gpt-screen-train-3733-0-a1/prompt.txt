QUESTION: Last year, Jorge planted corn on all of his 60 acres of property.  Typically, corn grown on good soil yields 400 bushels per acre, but in clay-rich soil, the yield is only half as much per acre as in good soil.  One-third of Jorge's 60 acres of land is clay-rich soil and the rest is good soil.  How many bushels of corn did Jorge's land yield last year?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total yield from good soil.
Jorge has 60 acres of land, with one-third being clay-rich soil. Therefore, the remaining two-thirds are good soil.
Total good soil acres = 60 acres * (2/3) = 40 acres
Yield per acre in good soil = 400 bushels
Total yield from good soil = 40 acres * 400 bushels/acre = 16,000 bushels

Step 2: Calculate the total yield from clay-rich soil.
Yield per acre in clay-rich soil = 400 bushels / 2 = 200 bushels
Total yield from clay-rich soil = 40 acres * 200 bushels/acre = 8,000 bushels

Step 3: Calculate the total yield from all the land.
Total yield = Yield from good soil + Yield from clay-rich soil
Total yield = 16,000 bushels + 8,000 bushels = 24,000 bushels

#### 24000
This is the total number of bushels of corn Jorge's land yielded last year.
