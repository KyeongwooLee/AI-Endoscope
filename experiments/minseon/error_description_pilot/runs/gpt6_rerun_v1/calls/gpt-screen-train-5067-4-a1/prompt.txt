QUESTION: John is laying the foundation for 3 homes. Each home needs a slab of concrete that is 100 feet by 100 feet by .5 feet. Concrete has a density of 150 pounds per cubic foot. A pound of concrete cost $.02 per pound. How much does the foundation cost?

1. **Volume Calculation**:
   - The concrete slab for one home is a rectangular prism with dimensions 100 feet by 100 feet by 0.5 feet.
   - Volume of one slab = \(100 \text{ feet} \times 100 \text{ feet} \times 0.5 \text{ feet} = 5000 \text{ cubic feet}\).

2. **Total Volume for 3 Homes**:
   - Total volume for 3 homes = \(3 \times 5000 \text{ cubic feet} = 15000 \text{ cubic feet}\).

3. **Weight Calculation**:
   - Concrete density is 150 pounds per cubic foot.
   - Weight of concrete for one home = \(5000 \text{ cubic feet} \times 150 \text{ pounds per cubic foot} = 750000 \text{ pounds}\).
   - Weight of concrete for 3 homes = \(3 \times 750000 \text{ pounds} = 2250000 \text{ pounds}\).

4. **Cost Calculation**:
   - Cost per pound of concrete is $0.02.
   - Total cost = \(2250000 \text{ pounds} \times \$0.02 \text{ per pound} = \$450000\).
