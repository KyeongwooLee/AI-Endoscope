QUESTION: Reginald is selling apples. He sells each apple for $1.25. He plans to use his profits to help repair his bike. His bike cost $80 and the repairs cost 25% of what his bike cost. After the repairs are paid for, 1/5 of the money he'd earned remains. How many apples did he sell?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the cost of bike repairs.
Repair cost = 25% of bike cost = 0.25 * $80 = $20

Step 2: Calculate the total amount earned before the bike repairs.
Total amount earned = bike cost + repair cost = $80 + $20 = $100

Step 3: Calculate the total amount earned after paying for bike repairs.
Total amount earned after repairs = total amount earned - repair cost = $100 - $20 = $80

Step 4: Calculate the number of apples sold.
Number of apples sold = total amount earned after repairs / price per apple = $80 / $1.25 = 64

#### 64
The number of apples sold is 64.
