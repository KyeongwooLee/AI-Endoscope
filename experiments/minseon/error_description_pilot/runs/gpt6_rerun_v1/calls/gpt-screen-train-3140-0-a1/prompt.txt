QUESTION: There are 10 rows of 15 chairs set up for the award ceremony. The first row is reserved for the awardees while the second and third rows are for the administrators and teachers. The last two rows are then reserved for the parents and the rest of the rows are for the students. If only 4/5 of the seats reserved for the students are occupied, how many vacant seats from the students can be given to the parents?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of chairs.
Total chairs = 10 rows * 15 chairs/row = 150 chairs

Step 2: Calculate the number of chairs reserved for the awardees.
Chairs for awardees = 1 row * 15 chairs/row = 15 chairs

Step 3: Calculate the number of chairs reserved for the administrators and teachers.
Chairs for administrators and teachers = 2 rows * 15 chairs/row = 30 chairs

Step 4: Calculate the number of chairs reserved for the parents.
Chairs for parents = 2 rows * 15 chairs/row = 30 chairs

Step 5: Calculate the number of chairs reserved for the students.
Chairs for students = Total chairs - Chairs for awardees - Chairs for administrators and teachers - Chairs for parents
Chairs for students = 150 - 15 - 30 - 30 = 85 chairs

Step 6: Calculate the number of occupied seats for the students.
Occupied seats for students = 4/5 * Chairs for students
Occupied seats for students = 4/5 * 85 = 68 seats

Step 7: Calculate the number of vacant seats for the students.
Vacant seats for students = Chairs for students - Occupied seats for students
Vacant seats for students = 85 - 68 = 17 seats
