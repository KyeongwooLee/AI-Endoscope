1. **Time to complete one job:**
   - It takes 3 men 1 hour to complete one job.

2. **Time to complete 5 jobs:**
   - If it takes 1 hour to complete 1 job, then for 5 jobs, it would take:
     \[
     1 \text{ hour/job} \times 5 \text{ jobs} = 5 \text{ hours}
     \]

3. **Total payment per hour:**
   - Each man is paid $10 per hour.

4. **Total earnings per man:**
   - The three men working together for 5 hours would earn:
     \[
     5 \text{ hours} \times 3 \text{ men} \times \$10/\text{hour} = 15 \times \$10 = \$150
     \]

5. **Total earnings for all three men:**
   - Since there are 3 men, the total earnings for all three men combined would be:
     \[
     3 \text{ men} \times \$150/\text{man} = \$450
