QUESTION: In eight years, Mr. Bernard will be 3 times as old as Luke is now. If Luke is 20, what is 10 years less than their average age?

In eight years, Mr. Bernard will be \( B + 8 \) years old.
According to the problem, in eight years, Mr. Bernard will be 3 times as old as Luke is now.
Luke's current age is 20, so in eight years, Luke will be 20 + 8 = 28 years old.
Therefore, \( B + 8 = 3 \times 28 \).

\[ B + 8 = 84 \]
\[ B = 84 - 8 \]
\[ B = 76 \]
