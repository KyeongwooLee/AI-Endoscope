QUESTION: In a spelling contest held in her school, Drew got 20 questions correct, winning her the competition. She got six questions wrong, and her competitor Carla got 14 questions correct, and twice as many questions wrong as the number of questions Drew got wrong. If each competitor was asked a different question, how many questions were asked in the competition?
SOLUTION: #### 30
Drew got 20 questions correct, which means she answered 20 questions.
Carla got 14 questions correct and twice as many wrong as Drew, which means Carla got 2 * 6 = 12 questions wrong.
So, Carla answered 14 + 12 = 26 questions.
Since each competitor was asked a different question, the total number of questions asked in the competition is 20 (Drew) + 26 (Carla) = 30 questions.
