QUESTION: Edward the plumber is replacing a pipe in a bathroom wall.  He needs to use 40 feet of copper pipe to complete the job.  For every 5 feet of pipe, Edward must use one tightening bolt, and for every bolt, he uses two washers.  He buys a bag of 20 washers for the job.  After he completes the bathroom job, how many washers will be remaining in the bag?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of tightening bolts needed.
Edward needs 1 bolt for every 5 feet of pipe. He has 40 feet of pipe.
40 feet / 5 feet per bolt = 8 bolts

Step 2: Calculate the number of washers needed.
For each bolt, Edward uses 2 washers.
8 bolts * 2 washers per bolt = 16 washers

Step 3: Determine the number of washers remaining.
Edward bought a bag of 20 washers.
16 washers used - 20 washers bought = -4 washers

Step 4: Calculate the number of washers remaining in the bag.
Since Edward used more washers than he bought, there will be a shortage.
-4 washers remaining in the bag

#### -4
Edward will have 4 washers remaining in the bag.
