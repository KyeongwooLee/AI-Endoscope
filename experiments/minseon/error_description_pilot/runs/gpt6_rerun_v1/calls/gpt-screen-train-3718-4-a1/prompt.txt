QUESTION: Tom opens an amusement park.  It cost $100,000 to open initially.  It also cost 1% of that to run per day.  He sells 150  tickets a day for $10 each.  How long will it take to make back his money?
SOLUTION: To solve this problem, let's break it down step by step:

1. **Initial Investment**: Tom spent $100,000 to open the amusement park.
2. **Daily Cost**: It costs 1% of the initial investment to run the park each day. 
   - Daily running cost = 1% of $100,000 = $1,000.
3. **Daily Revenue**: Tom sells 150 tickets a day for $10 each.
   - Daily revenue = 150 tickets * $10 per ticket = $1,500.

Now, let's calculate how many days it will take to recover the initial investment:

- Daily profit = Daily revenue - Daily running cost
  - Daily profit = $1,500 - $1,000 = $500.

- Number of days to break even = Initial investment / Daily profit
  - Number of days to break even = $100,000 / $500 = 200 days.

To find out how long it will take to make back his money, we add the initial investment to the daily profit:

- Total days to make back the money = Number of days to break even + Initial investment
  - Total days to make back the money = 200 days + $100,000

#### 200,100
It will take Tom 200 days and 100 cents to make back his money.
