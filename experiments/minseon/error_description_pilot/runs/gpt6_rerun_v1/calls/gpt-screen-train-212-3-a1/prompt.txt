QUESTION: Randy just turned 12 and started playing the piano. His friend Sheila told him about the 10,000-hour rule which says, after 10,000 hours of practice, you become an expert or master in your field. If Randy wants to become a piano expert before he is 20, how many hours a day will he need to practice if he practices every day, Monday – Friday, and takes two weeks off for vacation each year?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of practice days Randy will have in his lifetime.
Randy will practice from his 12th birthday to his 20th birthday, which is 8 years.
- Each year, Randy takes 2 weeks off for vacation.
- Total vacation days = 2 weeks/year * 2 years = 4 days.
- Practice days per year = 365 days/year - 4 days/vacation = 361 days/year.
- Total practice days = 361 days/year * 8 years = 2888 days.

Step 2: Calculate the total number of practice hours Randy needs to achieve expertise.
The 10,000-hour rule suggests that Randy needs to practice 10,000 hours to become an expert.

Step 3: Calculate the average number of hours Randy needs to practice per day.
- Total hours to practice = 10,000 hours.
- Total practice days = 2888 days.
- Average daily practice hours = 10,000 hours / 2888 days ≈ 34.72 hours/day.
