QUESTION: Max can mow the lawn in 40 minutes. If it takes him twice that long to fertilize the lawn, how long will it take him to both mow and fertilize the lawn?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the time it takes Max to fertilize the lawn.
Max takes 40 minutes to mow the lawn. It takes him twice as long to fertilize the lawn.
Time to fertilize = 2 × Time to mow
Time to fertilize = 2 × 40 minutes
Time to fertilize = 80 minutes

Step 2: Calculate the total time to mow and fertilize the lawn.
Total time = Time to mow + Time to fertilize
Total time = 40 minutes + 80 minutes
Total time = 120 minutes

Step 3: Convert the total time to hours.
1 hour = 60 minutes
Total time in hours = Total time / 60
Total time in hours = 120 minutes / 60
Total time in hours = 2 hours
