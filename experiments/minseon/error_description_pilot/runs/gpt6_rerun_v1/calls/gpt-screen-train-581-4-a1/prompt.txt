QUESTION: In ten years, I'll be twice my brother's age. The sum of our ages will then be 45 years old. How old am I now?
SOLUTION: Let's denote my current age as x and my brother's current age as y.

According to the problem:

1. In ten years, I'll be twice my brother's age: x + 10 = 2(y + 10)
2. The sum of our ages will then be 45 years old: x + y = 45

Now, we have a system of two equations with two variables:

x + 10 = 2y + 20
x + y = 45

Solving these equations step by step:

1. From the first equation, we can express y in terms of x:
   2y = x + 10 - 20
   y = (x + 10 - 20) / 2
   y = (x - 10) / 2

2. Substitute this expression for y into the second equation:
   x + (x - 10) / 2 = 45

3. Multiply both sides by 2 to clear the fraction:
   2x + (x - 10) = 90

4. Simplify and solve for x:
   2x + x - 10 = 90
   3x = 100
   x = 100 / 3
   x = 33.33

Therefore, my current age is 33.33 years old.
