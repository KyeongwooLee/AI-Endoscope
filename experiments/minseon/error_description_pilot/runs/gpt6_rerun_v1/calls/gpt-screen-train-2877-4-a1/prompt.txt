QUESTION: Stuart is going on a cross-country trip and wants to find the fastest route. On one route the total distance is 1500 miles and the average speed is 75 MPH. On the second trip, the total distance is 750 but the average speed is 25 MPH. How long does his trip take if he goes on the fastest route?
SOLUTION: To determine the fastest route, we need to calculate the travel time for both routes and compare them.

**Route 1:**
- Total distance: 1500 miles
- Average speed: 75 MPH

\[ \text{Time for Route 1} = \frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Average speed}} = \frac{1500 \text{ miles}}{75 \text{ MPH}} \]

\[ \text{Time for Route 1} = 20 \text{ hours} \]

**Route 2:**
- Total distance: 750 miles
- Average speed: 25 MPH

\[ \text{Time for Route 2} = \frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Average speed}} = \frac{750 \text{ miles}}{25 \text{ MPH}} \]

\[ \text{Time for Route 2} = 30 \text{ hours} \]

Comparing the two routes:
- Route 1: 20 hours
- Route 2: 30 hours

**Conclusion:**
The fastest route is Route 1, taking 20 hours.
