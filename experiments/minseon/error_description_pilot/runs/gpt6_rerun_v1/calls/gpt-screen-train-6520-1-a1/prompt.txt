QUESTION: Doris earns $20 per hour by babysitting. She needs to earn at least $1200 for her monthly expenses. She can babysit for 3 hours every weekday and 5 hours on a Saturday.  How many weeks does it take for Doris to earn enough to cover her monthly expenses?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate Doris's daily earnings from babysitting.
Doris earns $20 per hour. She babysits for 3 hours on weekdays and 5 hours on Saturdays.
Weekday earnings: \(3 \text{ hours/day} \times \$20/\text{hour} = \$60/\text{day}\)
Saturday earnings: \(5 \text{ hours/day} \times \$20/\text{hour} = \$100/\text{day}\)
Total daily earnings: \(\$60 + \$100 = \$160\)

Step 2: Calculate Doris's weekly earnings.
Doris works 5 weekdays and 1 Saturday in a week.
Weekly earnings: \(5 \text{ days} \times \$160/\text{day} = \$800\)

Step 3: Calculate the number of weeks needed to earn at least $1200.
Number of weeks needed: \(\frac{\$1200}{\$800/\text{week}} = 1.5 \text{ weeks}\)

#### 1.5
Doris needs 1.5 weeks to earn enough to cover her monthly expenses.
