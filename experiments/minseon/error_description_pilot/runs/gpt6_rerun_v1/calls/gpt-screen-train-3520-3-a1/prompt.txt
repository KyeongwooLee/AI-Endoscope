QUESTION: Haley is getting ready to watch a comet fly over her house. She spends two hours shopping for a telescope, half an hour getting everything set up in the backyard, three times the setup time making snacks, and 20 minutes watching the comet. What percentage of the total time she spent on all those activities was spent watching the comet, rounded to the nearest percent?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total time spent on shopping, setup, making snacks, and watching the comet.
- Shopping: 2 hours
- Setup: 0.5 hours
- Making snacks: 3 times the setup time = 3 * 0.5 = 1.5 hours
- Watching the comet: 20 minutes = 20/60 = 1/3 hours

Step 2: Convert all times to hours for consistency.
- Total time: 2 + 0.5 + 1.5 + 1/3 = 2 + 0.5 + 1.5 + 0.333 = 4.333 hours

Step 3: Calculate the percentage of time spent watching the comet.
\[ \text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{\text{Time watching the comet}}{\text{Total time}} \right) \times 100 \]
\[ \text{Percentage} = \left( \frac{1/3}{4.333} \right) \times 100 \approx 2.31\% \]

Step 4: Round to the nearest percent.
\[ \text{Percentage} \approx 2\% \]

#### 2
The percentage of the total time Haley spent watching the comet is 2%.
