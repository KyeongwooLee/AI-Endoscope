\[ 5x + 5(x + 6) = 77 \]

\[ 5x + 5x + 30 = 77 \]
\[ 10x + 30 = 77 \]

\[ 10x = 47 \]

Then, solve for \( x \) by dividing both sides by 10:

\[ x = 4.7 \]

\[ x + 6 = 4.7 + 6 = 10.7 \]
