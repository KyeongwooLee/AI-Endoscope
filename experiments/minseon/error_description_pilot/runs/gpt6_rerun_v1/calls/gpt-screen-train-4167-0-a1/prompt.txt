Andrew invites two more friends, so he asks for 6 donuts for each of them.
6 + 6 = 12 donuts

Step 3: Calculate the number of donuts for Andrew.
Andrew's mother wants to buy one more donut for each of Andrew's friends.
12 + 1 = 13 donuts

Step 4: Calculate the total number of donuts for Andrew's mother.
Andrew's mother needs to buy the same amount of donuts for Andrew as for the other friends.
