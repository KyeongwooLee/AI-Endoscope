QUESTION: Harry decided to buy some balloons for his friend's birthday party. One balloon costs $0.5, and a pack of 10 balloons is cheaper and costs only $3. Finally, Harry wants to bring to the party exactly 14 balloons. How much did he need to pay for them?
SOLUTION: To determine how much Harry needs to pay for 14 balloons, we can break down the problem into two parts: buying individual balloons and buying packs of 10 balloons.

1. **Calculate the cost of individual balloons:**
   - Harry wants 14 balloons.
   - Each individual balloon costs $0.5.
   - Therefore, the cost for 14 individual balloons is:
     \[
     14 \times 0.5 = 7 \text{ dollars}
     \]

2. **Calculate the cost of packs of 10 balloons:**
   - Each pack of 10 balloons costs $3.
   - Harry needs 14 balloons, which is 2 packs of 10 balloons (since 10 + 10 = 20, but he only needs 14).
   - Therefore, the cost for 2 packs of 10 balloons is:
     \[
     2 \times 3 = 6 \text{ dollars}
     \]

3. **Calculate the total cost:**
   - The total cost is the sum of the cost of individual balloons and the cost of packs of 10 balloons:
     \[
     7 + 6 = 13 \text{ dollars}
     \]
