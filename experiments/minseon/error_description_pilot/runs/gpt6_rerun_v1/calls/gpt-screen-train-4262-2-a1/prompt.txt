QUESTION: Barry and his friend, Jim, went horseback riding across central Texas. They traveled at 5 miles per hour for 7 hours, and then stopped for the evening. On the third and final day, they traveled for 5 hours at 7 miles per hour.  In total, how far did they travel, in miles, during their horseback riding trip?

Distance = Speed × Time
Distance = 5 miles/hour × 7 hours
Distance = 35 miles

Step 2: Calculate the distance traveled on the second day.
First part of the second day: 6 miles/hour × 6 hours
Second part of the second day: 3 miles/hour × 3 hours
Distance = 36 miles

Step 3: Calculate the total distance traveled.
Total distance = Distance on the first day + Distance on the second day
Total distance = 35 miles + 36 miles
