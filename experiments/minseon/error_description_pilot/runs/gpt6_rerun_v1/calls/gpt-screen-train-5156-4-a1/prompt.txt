QUESTION: A man used to have 39 cows but last year 25 of them died and he sold 6 of them. This year the number of the cows increased by 24 and the man bought 43 more. His friend gave him 8 cows as a gift. How many cows does the man have now?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of cows the man had after last year's events.
- Initial number of cows: 39
- Cows died: 25
- Cows sold: 6
- Cows increased by: 24
- Cows bought: 43

\[ \text{Cows after last year} = 39 - 25 + 6 + 24 + 43 \]

Step 2: Simplify the expression to find the number of cows after last year.

\[ \text{Cows after last year} = 39 - 25 + 6 + 24 + 43 \]
\[ \text{Cows after last year} = 14 + 6 + 24 + 43 \]
\[ \text{Cows after last year} = 20 + 24 + 43 \]
\[ \text{Cows after last year} = 44 + 43 \]
\[ \text{Cows after last year} = 87 \]

#### 87
This is the number of cows the man had after last year.

Step 3: Calculate the number of cows the man has now, after this year's events.
- Cows given as gift: 8

\[ \text{Total cows now} = 87 + 8 \]

Step 4: Simplify the expression to find the total number of cows now.

\[ \text{Total cows now} = 87 + 8 \]
\[ \text{Total cows now} = 95 \]

#### 95
This is the number of cows the man has now.
