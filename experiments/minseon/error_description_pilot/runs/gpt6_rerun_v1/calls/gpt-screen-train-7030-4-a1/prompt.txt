QUESTION: Sally was at a self-serve candy shop where you scoop candy from the bins and pay based on the weight. She scoops 32 cherry sours into a bag. Then she gets a scoop of lemon sours, and the ratio of cherry sours to lemon sours is 4:5. Then she gets a scoop of orange sours, and they make up 25% of the sours in the bag. How many sours does she have in total?
SOLUTION: To find the total number of sours Sally has, we start by defining variables for the quantities of each type of sour. Let \( C \) be the number of cherry sours, \( L \) be the number of lemon sours, and \( O \) be the number of orange sours.

Given:
1. Sally has 32 cherry sours: \( C = 32 \)
2. The ratio of cherry sours to lemon sours is 4:5: \( \frac{C}{L} = \frac{4}{5} \)
3. Orange sours make up 25% of the total number of sours: \( O = 0.25(C + L + O) \)

First, solve for \( L \) using the ratio:
\[
\frac{C}{L} = \frac{4}{5} \implies L = \frac{5C}{4}
\]
Substitute \( C = 32 \):
\[
L = \frac{5 \times 32}{4} = \frac{160}{4} = 40
\]

Next, solve for \( O \) using the total number of sours:
\[
O = 0.25(C + L + O)
\]
Let \( T \) be the total number of sours:
\[
T = C + L + O
\]
Substitute \( C = 32 \) and \( L = 40 \):
\[
T = 32 + 40 + O
\]
We need to find \( O \) first:
\[
O = 0.25(T - 72)
\]
\[
O = 0.25(32 + 40 + O - 72)
\]
\[
O = 0.25(72 - 72 + O)
\]
\[
O = 0.25O
\]
\[
O = 2
\]

Finally, find the total number of sours:
\[
T = 32 + 40 + 2 = 74
\]

#### 74
The total number of sours Sally has is 74.
