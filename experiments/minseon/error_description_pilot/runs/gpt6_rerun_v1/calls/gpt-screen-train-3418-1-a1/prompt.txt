QUESTION: Stella collects stamps. She counted 50 pages in her collector's album. There are 5 rows of 30 stamps in each of the first 10 pages of her album. The rest of the pages each have 50 stamps. How many stamps are in Stella's album?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of stamps in the first 10 pages.
50 pages * 5 rows/page * 30 stamps/row = 750 stamps

Step 2: Calculate the total number of stamps in the remaining pages.
50 pages - 10 pages = 40 pages
40 pages * 50 stamps/page = 2000 stamps

Step 3: Calculate the total number of stamps in the entire album.
750 stamps + 2000 stamps = 2750 stamps

#### 2750
The total number of stamps in Stella's album is 2750.
