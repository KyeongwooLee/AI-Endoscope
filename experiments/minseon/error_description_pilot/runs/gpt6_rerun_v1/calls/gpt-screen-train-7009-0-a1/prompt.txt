QUESTION: Pablo’s mother agrees to pay him one cent for every page he reads. He plans to save the money for some candy. Pablo always checks out books that are exactly 150 pages. SOLUTION: To find out how many books Pablo read, we need to follow these steps:

1. Determine how much money Pablo earned from reading the books.
2. Calculate how many books he read based on the amount of money he earned.

Step 1: Calculate the total money Pablo earned from reading the books.
Pablo bought $15 worth of candy and had $3 leftover. So, the total money he earned is:
$15 (candy) + $3 (leftover) = $18

Step 2: Calculate how many books Pablo read.
Pablo earns 1 cent for every page he reads. Since each book is 150 pages, the number of books he read is:
$18 (total money earned) / $0.01 (cost per book) = 1800 books
