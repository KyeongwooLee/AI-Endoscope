QUESTION: It takes Nissa 10 seconds to clip each of her cats' nails. 90 seconds to clean each of her ears, and 5 minutes to shampoo her. If the cat has four claws on each foot, how many seconds does grooming her cat take total?
SOLUTION: To find the total time taken to groom the cat, we need to calculate the time spent on each task and then sum them up.

1. Time to clip the nails: 10 seconds per claw * 4 claws on each foot * 4 feet = 160 seconds
2. Time to clean the ears: 90 seconds per cat
3. Time to shampoo the cat: 5 minutes = 5 * 60 seconds = 300 seconds

Total time = Time to clip nails + Time to clean ears + Time to shampoo
Total time = 160 seconds + 90 seconds + 300 seconds
Total time = 550 seconds
