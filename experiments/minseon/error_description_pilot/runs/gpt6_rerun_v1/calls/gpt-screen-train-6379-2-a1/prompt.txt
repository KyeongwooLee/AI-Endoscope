QUESTION: Steve writes a letter every 3rd day to his friend.  He spends 20 minutes writing the letters.  It takes 10 minutes to write 1 page.  He also writes a long letter at the end of the month which he puts more thought into.  It takes twice as long per page but he spends 80 minutes writing.  How many pages does he write a month?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of letters Steve writes in a month.
He writes a letter every 3rd day. There are 30 days in a month.
Number of letters = 30 / 3 = 10 letters

Step 2: Calculate the total time Steve spends writing letters.
Each letter takes 20 minutes to write.
Total time = 10 letters * 20 minutes/letter = 200 minutes

Step 3: Calculate the number of pages Steve writes in the regular letters.
Each page takes 10 minutes to write.
Number of pages in regular letters = 200 minutes / 10 minutes/page = 20 pages

Step 4: Calculate the time Steve spends on the long letter.
It takes twice as long per page as a regular letter, so 20 * 2 = 40 minutes per page.
Total time for the long letter = 80 minutes
Number of pages in the long letter = 80 minutes / 40 minutes/page = 2 pages

Step 5: Calculate the total number of pages Steve writes in a month.
Total pages = 20 pages (regular letters) + 2 pages (long letter) = 22 pages
