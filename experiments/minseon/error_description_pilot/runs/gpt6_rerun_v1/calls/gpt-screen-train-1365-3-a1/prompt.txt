QUESTION: A portable computer drive has enough kilobytes of storage space to store 2000 photos. Each photo is 1.5 kilobytes in size. How many 200-kilobyte videos can it store if it already has 400 photos on it?
SOLUTION: To solve this problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. Calculate the total storage space used by the 400 photos.
2. Subtract this space from the total storage space to find the remaining capacity.
3. Determine how many 200-kilobyte videos can be stored in the remaining capacity.

Step 1: Calculate the total storage space used by the 400 photos.
Each photo is 1.5 kilobytes in size, so for 400 photos:
\[ 400 \text{ photos} \times 1.5 \text{ kilobytes/photo} = 600 \text{ kilobytes} \]

Step 2: Subtract this space from the total storage space.
The total storage space is 2000 kilobytes, so:
\[ 2000 \text{ kilobytes} - 600 \text{ kilobytes} = 1400 \text{ kilobytes} \]

Step 3: Determine how many 200-kilobyte videos can be stored in the remaining capacity.
\[ 1400 \text{ kilobytes} \div 200 \text{ kilobytes/video} = 7 \text{ videos} \]
