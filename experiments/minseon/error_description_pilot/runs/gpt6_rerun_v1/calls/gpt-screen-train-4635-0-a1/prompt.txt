QUESTION: Johnny buys 15 packs of colored pencils for his class. Each pack has a red, yellow, and green pencil inside. When he gets home he notices that 3 of the packs have two extra red pencils inside. How many red colored pencils did Johnny buy?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the number of red pencils in the 12 packs that do not have extra red pencils.
Each of these 12 packs contains 1 red pencil.
12 packs * 1 red pencil per pack = 12 red pencils

Step 2: Determine the number of red pencils in the 3 packs that have extra red pencils.
Each of these 3 packs contains 2 red pencils.
3 packs * 2 red pencils per pack = 6 red pencils

Step 3: Add the number of red pencils from both types of packs to get the total number of red pencils Johnny bought.
12 red pencils + 6 red pencils = 18 red pencils
