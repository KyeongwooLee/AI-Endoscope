QUESTION: Kara has to drink 4 ounces of water every time she takes her medication. Her medication instructions are to take one tablet three times a day. She followed the instructions for one week, but in the second week, she forgot twice on one day. How many ounces of water did she drink with her medication over those two weeks?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total ounces of water Kara drinks in one day without any forgetfulness.
She drinks 4 ounces of water with each tablet and takes the tablet 3 times a day.
4 ounces/tablet * 3 tablets/day = 12 ounces/day

Step 2: Calculate the total ounces of water Kara drinks in one week without any forgetfulness.
12 ounces/day * 7 days/week = 84 ounces/week

Step 3: Calculate the total ounces of water Kara drinks in the first week.
84 ounces/week

Step 4: Calculate the total ounces of water Kara drinks in the second week.
She forgot twice on one day, so she only took the tablet 1 time that day.
12 ounces/day * 5 days/week = 60 ounces/week

Step 5: Calculate the total ounces of water Kara drinks in the two weeks.
84 ounces/week + 60 ounces/week = 144 ounces
