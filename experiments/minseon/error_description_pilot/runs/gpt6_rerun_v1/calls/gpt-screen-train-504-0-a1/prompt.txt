QUESTION: From March to August, Sam made $460 doing 23 hours of yard work. However, from September to February, Sam was only able to work for 8 hours. If Sam is saving up to buy a video game console that costs $600 and has already spent $340 to fix his car, how many more hours does he need to work before he can buy the video game console?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate Sam's hourly wage.
Total earnings from March to August = $460
Total hours worked from March to August = 23 hours
Hourly wage = Total earnings / Total hours
Hourly wage = $460 / 23 hours
Hourly wage = $20 per hour

Step 2: Calculate Sam's earnings from September to February.
Total hours worked from September to February = 8 hours
Earnings from September to February = Hourly wage * Total hours
Earnings from September to February = $20 * 8 hours
Earnings from September to February = $160

Step 3: Calculate Sam's total earnings.
Total earnings = Earnings from March to August + Earnings from September to February
Total earnings = $460 + $160
Total earnings = $620

Step 4: Calculate the remaining amount needed to buy the video game console.
Cost of video game console = $600
Amount already spent = $340
Remaining amount needed = Cost of video game console - Amount already spent
Remaining amount needed = $600 - $340
Remaining amount needed = $260

Step 5: Calculate the number of hours Sam needs to work to earn the remaining amount.
Total earnings needed = $260
Hourly wage = $20 per hour
Number of hours needed = Total earnings needed / Hourly wage
Number of hours needed = $260 / $20
Number of hours needed = 13 hours
