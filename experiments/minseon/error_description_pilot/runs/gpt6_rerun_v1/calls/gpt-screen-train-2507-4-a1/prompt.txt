QUESTION: Sadie, Ariana and Sarah are running a relay race. Each part of the race is a different length and covers different terrain. It takes Sadie 2 hours to run through the forest at an average speed of 3 miles per hour. Ariana sprints across the open field on her section at 6 miles per hour for half an hour. If Sarah runs along the beach at four miles per hour and their total time for the race is four and half hours, what is the total distance of the race?
SOLUTION: To find the total distance of the race, we need to calculate the distance each runner covers and then sum them up.

1. **Sadie's Distance:**
   - Time: 2 hours
   - Speed: 3 miles per hour
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 3 miles/hour × 2 hours = 6 miles

2. **Ariana's Distance:**
   - Speed: 6 miles per hour
   - Time: 0.5 hours
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 6 miles/hour × 0.5 hours = 3 miles

3. **Sarah's Distance:**
   - Sarah runs along the beach at 4 miles per hour
   - Total time for the race = 4.5 hours
   - Time for Sarah = Total time - Time for Sadie and Ariana
   - Time for Sarah = 4.5 hours - 2 hours = 2.5 hours
   - Distance = Speed × Time
   - Distance = 4 miles/hour × 2.5 hours = 10 miles

4. **Total Distance of the Race:**
   - Total distance = Distance covered by Sadie + Distance covered by Ariana + Distance covered by Sarah
   - Total distance = 6 miles + 3 miles + 10 miles = 19 miles
