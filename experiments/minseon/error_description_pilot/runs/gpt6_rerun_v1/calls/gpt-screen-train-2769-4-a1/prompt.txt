QUESTION: Linda makes and sells necklaces at craft fairs. At her most recent fair she sold 4 necklaces and 8 rings for a total of $80. If each necklace costs $12, how much does each ring cost?
SOLUTION: #### 5
Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the necklaces sold.
Each necklace costs $12 and Linda sold 4 necklaces.
\[ 4 \times 12 = 48 \]

Step 2: Subtract the cost of the necklaces from the total amount earned to find the cost of the rings.
Total amount earned is $80.
Subtract the cost of the necklaces from the total amount:
\[ 80 - 48 = 32 \]

Step 3: Determine the cost of each ring.
Linda sold 8 rings for a total of $32.
Divide the total cost of the rings by the number of rings:
\[ 32 \div 8 = 4 \]

Therefore, the cost of each ring is $4.
