QUESTION: Cindy tosses 5 dimes into the wishing pond.  Eric flips 3 quarters into the pond.  Garrick throws in 8 nickels.  Ivy then drops 60 pennies in.  If Eric dips his hands into the water and pulls out a quarter, how much money, in cents, did they put into the pond?
SOLUTION: To solve this problem, we need to calculate the total amount of money each person put into the pond and then sum these amounts.

1. Cindy tosses 5 dimes into the pond.
   - 1 dime = 10 cents
   - 5 dimes = 5 * 10 = 50 cents

2. Eric flips 3 quarters into the pond.
   - 1 quarter = 25 cents
   - 3 quarters = 3 * 25 = 75 cents

3. Garrick throws in 8 nickels into the pond.
   - 1 nickel = 5 cents
   - 8 nickels = 8 * 5 = 40 cents

4. Ivy drops 60 pennies into the pond.
   - 1 penny = 1 cent
   - 60 pennies = 60 * 1 = 60 cents

Now, let's sum up the total amount of money they put into the pond:

Total = Cindy's money + Eric's money + Garrick's money + Ivy's money
Total = 50 cents + 75 cents + 40 cents + 60 cents
Total = 225 cents
