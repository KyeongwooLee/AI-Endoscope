SOLUTION: #### 20
To find out how long it will take Erin to serve all the soup, we first need to determine the total amount of soup in ounces. Since there are 128 ounces in a gallon and the pot has 6 gallons of soup, we multiply 128 by 6:

\[ 128 \times 6 = 768 \text{ ounces} \]

Next, we need to determine how many bowls of soup can be served with 768 ounces. Since each bowl contains 10 ounces, we divide 768 by 10:

\[ 768 \div 10 = 76.8 \text{ bowls} \]

Erin can serve 5 bowls per minute, so we divide the number of bowls by 5 to find out how many minutes it will take to serve all the soup:

\[ 76.8 \div 5 = 15.36 \text{ minutes} \]

Since we need to round to the nearest minute, we round 15.36 to 15 minutes. Therefore, the time it will take Erin to serve all the soup is 15 minutes.
