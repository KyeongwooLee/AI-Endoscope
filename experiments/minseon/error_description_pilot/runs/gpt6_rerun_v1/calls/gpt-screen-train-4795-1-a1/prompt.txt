QUESTION: James does chores around the class.  There are 3 bedrooms, 1 living room, and 2 bathrooms to clean.  The bedrooms each take 20 minutes to clean.  The living room takes as long as the 3 bedrooms combined.  The bathroom takes twice as long as the living room.  He also cleans the outside which takes twice as long as cleaning the house.  He splits the chores with his 2 siblings who are just as fast as him.  How long, in hours, does he work?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the time to clean all the bedrooms.
3 bedrooms * 20 minutes per bedroom = 60 minutes

Step 2: Calculate the time to clean the living room.
Living room takes as long as the 3 bedrooms combined.
60 minutes (bedrooms) * 3 = 180 minutes

Step 3: Calculate the time to clean the bathrooms.
Bathroom takes twice as long as the living room.
180 minutes (living room) * 2 = 360 minutes

Step 4: Calculate the total time to clean the house.
60 minutes (bedrooms) + 180 minutes (living room) + 360 minutes (bathrooms) = 600 minutes

Step 5: Calculate the time to clean the outside.
Outside takes twice as long as cleaning the house.
600 minutes (house) * 2 = 1200 minutes

Step 6: Calculate the total time to clean everything.
600 minutes (house) + 1200 minutes (outside) = 1800 minutes

Step 7: Calculate the time per person.
1800 minutes / 4 people = 450 minutes

Step 8: Convert minutes to hours.
450 minutes / 60 minutes per hour = 7.5 hours
