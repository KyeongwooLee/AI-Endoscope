SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total cost of feeding the cattle.

Total cost of feeding = $400 * 100 = $40,000
Step 2: Calculate the total cost of buying and feeding the cattle.

Step 3: Calculate the total weight of the cattle.
Total weight = 100 * 1000 = 100,000 pounds
Step 4: Calculate the total revenue from selling the cattle.
Total revenue = 100,000 * $2 = $200,000
Step 5: Calculate the profit.
Profit = Total revenue - Total cost = $200,000 - $80,000 = $120,000
