QUESTION: Frank bakes two trays of cookies per day, for 6 days. Frank eats one cookie each day to make sure they taste good. Ted comes over on the sixth day and eats 4 cookies. If each tray makes 12 cookies, how many cookies are left when Ted leaves?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of trays baked over 6 days.
Frank bakes 2 trays per day for 6 days.
2 trays/day * 6 days = 12 trays

Step 2: Calculate the total number of cookies baked.
Each tray makes 12 cookies.
12 cookies/tray * 12 trays = 144 cookies

Step 3: Calculate the number of cookies eaten by Frank.
Frank eats 1 cookie each day for 6 days.
1 cookie/day * 6 days = 6 cookies

Step 4: Calculate the number of cookies left after Frank eats.
Total cookies baked - Cookies eaten by Frank = Cookies left
144 cookies - 6 cookies = 138 cookies

#### 138
Frank has 138 cookies left when Ted leaves.
