QUESTION: Each week, Paul has 2 hours of homework on weeknights and 5 hours for the entire weekend. This week Paul has practice 2 nights out of the week and can't do any homework those nights. How many hours of homework does he have to average for the other nights to get his week's homework done?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total homework hours for the week.
- Weeknights: 2 hours each night for 5 nights
- Weekend: 5 hours

Total homework hours = (2 hours/night * 5 nights) + 5 hours = 10 + 5 = 15 hours

Step 2: Subtract the hours Paul can't do homework due to practice.
- Paul can't do homework on 2 nights

Hours Paul can do homework = 15 hours - 2 hours = 13 hours

Step 3: Determine the number of nights Paul has to do homework.
- There are 5 nights in a week and 2 are for practice

Nights left for homework = 5 nights - 2 nights = 3 nights

Step 4: Calculate the average number of hours of homework Paul needs to do each night.
- Total hours for homework: 13 hours
- Number of nights: 3 nights

Average hours of homework per night = 13 hours / 3 nights = 4.33 hours/night
