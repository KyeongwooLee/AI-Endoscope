QUESTION: Antonio is preparing a meal of spaghetti and meatballs for his family.  His recipe for meatballs calls for 1/8 of a pound of hamburger per meatball.  Antonio has 8 family members, including himself.  If he uses 4 pounds of hamburger to make meatballs, and each member of the family eats an equal number of meatballs, how many meatballs will Antonio eat?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of meatballs Antonio can make with 4 pounds of hamburger.
Step 2: Determine how many meatballs each family member will get.
Step 3: Calculate the number of meatballs Antonio will eat.

#### 48
#### Step-by-step reasoning:
Step 1: 
- Antonio has 4 pounds of hamburger.
- Each meatball requires 1/8 pound of hamburger.
- Total number of meatballs = Total hamburger ÷ Meatball per hamburger = 4 ÷ (1/8) = 4 * 8 = 32 meatballs.

Step 2: 
- There are 8 family members.
- Each family member will get the same number of meatballs.
- Number of meatballs per person = Total meatballs ÷ Number of family members = 32 ÷ 8 = 4 meatballs per person.

Step 3: 
- Antonio will eat 4 meatballs.
