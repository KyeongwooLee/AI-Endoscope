QUESTION: Melany has to fence a 5000 feet square field with wire mesh. If one foot of wire mesh is sold at $30, and she had $120000, how many feet of the field will not be fenced?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the perimeter of the square field.
Perimeter = 4 * side length
We know the perimeter is 5000 feet.
Step 2: Calculate the total cost of wire mesh needed.
Cost per foot = $30
Total cost = Perimeter * Cost per foot
Step 3: Calculate the amount of money Melany has left after purchasing wire mesh.
Money left = Initial amount - Total cost
Step 4: Calculate the remaining feet of the field that will not be fenced.
Remaining feet = Perimeter - Money left

Remaining feet = 4 * 5000 - (4 * 5000 - 120000 / 30)
