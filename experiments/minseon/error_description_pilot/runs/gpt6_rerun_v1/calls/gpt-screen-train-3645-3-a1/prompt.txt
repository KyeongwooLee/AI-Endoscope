QUESTION: Geordie takes a toll road on his drive to work and back every day of his five-day workweek. The toll is $12.50 per car or $7 per motorcycle. Both his car and his motorcycle get 35 miles per gallon of gas and the commute to work is 14 miles. Gas costs him $3.75 per gallon. Geordie drives his car to work three times a week and his motorcycle to work twice a week. How many dollars does he spend driving to work and back on the same route in a week?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the daily toll for both car and motorcycle.
- Car toll: $12.50
- Motorcycle toll: $7

Step 2: Calculate the total toll for the week.
- Car toll for the week: $12.50 * 3 days = $37.50
- Motorcycle toll for the week: $7 * 2 days = $14

Step 3: Calculate the total gas cost for the week.
- Gas cost for the car: $3.75 * 14 miles / 35 miles per gallon * 3 days
  = $3.75 * (14 / 35) * 3
  = $3.75 * 0.4 * 3
  = $4.50

- Gas cost for the motorcycle: $3.75 * 14 miles / 35 miles per gallon * 2 days
  = $3.75 * (14 / 35) * 2
  = $3.75 * 0.4 * 2
  = $3.00

Step 4: Calculate the total weekly driving cost.
- Total cost: $4.50 + $3.00 = $7.50

#### 7.50
Geordie spends $7.50 driving to work and back on the same route in a week.
