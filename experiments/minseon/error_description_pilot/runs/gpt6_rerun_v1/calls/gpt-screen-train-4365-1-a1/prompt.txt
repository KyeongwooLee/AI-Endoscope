QUESTION: There are three times as many misses as hits in an MLB game. If the number of misses is 50, how many hits and misses are made in the game?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Let's define the variables.
- Let \( H \) represent the number of hits.
- Let \( M \) represent the number of misses.

Step 2: According to the problem, we know that the number of misses is three times the number of hits.
\[ M = 3H \]

Step 3: We are given that the number of misses is 50.
\[ M = 50 \]

Step 4: Substitute \( M \) with 50 in the equation \( M = 3H \).
\[ 50 = 3H \]

Step 5: Solve for \( H \).
\[ H = \frac{50}{3} \]
\[ H = 16.67 \]

Final Answer:
#### 16.67
The number of hits and misses made in the game are 16.67 hits and 50 misses respectively.
