QUESTION: Lilith is trying to break the world record for largest cap collection. She collects 3 caps per month in the first year, and 5 caps per month after the first year. Each Christmas, she also receives 40 caps from friends and family. She estimates that each year, she loses 15 of the caps she has collected. If Lilith has been collecting for 5 years, how many caps has she collected so far?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of caps collected in the first year.
Caps collected in the first year = 3 caps/month * 12 months/year
Caps collected in the first year = 36 caps/year

Step 2: Calculate the number of caps collected in the subsequent years.
Caps collected in the subsequent years = 5 caps/month * 12 months/year * 4 years
Caps collected in the subsequent years = 240 caps/year

Step 3: Calculate the total number of caps collected before Christmas gifts.
Total caps before Christmas gifts = Caps collected in the first year + Caps collected in the subsequent years
Total caps before Christmas gifts = 36 caps + 240 caps
Total caps before Christmas gifts = 276 caps

Step 4: Calculate the total number of caps received from Christmas gifts.
Total caps from Christmas gifts = 40 caps/year * 5 years
Total caps from Christmas gifts = 200 caps

Step 5: Calculate the total number of caps before accounting for lost caps.
Total caps before lost caps = Total caps before Christmas gifts + Total caps from Christmas gifts
Total caps before lost caps = 276 caps + 200 caps
Total caps before lost caps = 476 caps

Step 6: Calculate the total number of caps lost over the 5 years.
Total caps lost = 15 caps/year * 5 years
Total caps lost = 75 caps

Step 7: Calculate the total number of caps collected after accounting for lost caps.
Total caps collected = Total caps before lost caps - Total caps lost
Total caps collected = 476 caps - 75 caps
Total caps collected = 391 caps

#### 391
Lilith has collected 391 caps so far.
