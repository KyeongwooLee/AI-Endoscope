QUESTION: The population of an area starts at 100,000 people.  It increases by 60% over 10 years due to birth.  In that same time, 2000 people leave per year from emigration and 2500 people come in per year from immigration.  How many people are in the area at the end of 10 years?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the increase in population due to birth over 10 years.
Increase in population due to birth = 100,000 * 60% = 100,000 * 0.60 = 60,000 people

Step 2: Calculate the net change in population over 10 years.
Net change in population = Increase in population due to birth - (2000 people leave per year - 2500 people come in per year)
Net change in population = 60,000 - (2000 - 2500) = 60,000 - (-500) = 60,000 + 500 = 60,500 people

Step 3: Calculate the population at the end of 10 years.
Population at the end of 10 years = Initial population + Net change in population
Population at the end of 10 years = 100,000 + 60,500 = 160,500 people

The population of the area at the end of 10 years is 160,500 people.
