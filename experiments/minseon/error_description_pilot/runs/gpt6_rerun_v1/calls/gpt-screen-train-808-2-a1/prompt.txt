QUESTION: Carla is taking attendance for her sixth-grade class. She knows that 2 students are in the restroom, and one less than three times that number of students are absent. If there are four rows with six desks each that are all 2/3 full, how many students does Carla teach, including the ones in and out of the classroom?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the number of students in the restroom.
Number of students in the restroom = 2

Step 2: Calculate the number of absent students.
Absent students = 3 * Number of students in the restroom - 1
Absent students = 3 * 2 - 1
Absent students = 6 - 1
Absent students = 5

Step 3: Find the number of desks occupied in the classroom.
Occupied desks = Number of rows * Number of desks per row * Fraction of desks occupied
Occupied desks = 4 * 6 * 2/3
Occupied desks = 24 * 2/3
Occupied desks = 16

Step 4: Calculate the total number of students in the classroom.
Total students in the classroom = Number of occupied desks + Number of students in the restroom
Total students in the classroom = 16 + 2
Total students in the classroom = 18

Step 5: Determine the total number of students Carla teaches.
Total students = Students in the restroom + Absent students + Total students in the classroom
Total students = 2 + 5 + 18
Total students = 25

#### 25
The total number of students Carla teaches, including those in and out of the classroom, is 25.
