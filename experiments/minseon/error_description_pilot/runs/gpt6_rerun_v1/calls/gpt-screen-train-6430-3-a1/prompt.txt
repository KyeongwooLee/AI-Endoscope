QUESTION: On the weekend, Tony will walk to the store. On weekdays, he runs to the store. When he walks, he goes 2 MPH. When he runs he goes 10 MPH. The store is 4 miles away. If he goes on Sunday, Tuesday, and Thursday, what is the average time in minutes that he spends to get to the store?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the time Tony spends on Sunday when he walks to the store.
- Distance to the store: 4 miles
- Speed on Sunday: 2 mph
- Time on Sunday: 4 miles ÷ 2 mph = 2 hours

Step 2: Calculate the time Tony spends on Tuesday when he runs to the store.
- Distance to the store: 4 miles
- Speed on Tuesday: 10 mph
- Time on Tuesday: 4 miles ÷ 10 mph = 0.4 hours

Step 3: Calculate the time Tony spends on Thursday when he runs to the store.
- Distance to the store: 4 miles
- Speed on Thursday: 10 mph
- Time on Thursday: 4 miles ÷ 10 mph = 0.4 hours

Step 4: Calculate the total time spent on all three days.
- Total time = Time on Sunday + Time on Tuesday + Time on Thursday
- Total time = 2 hours + 0.4 hours + 0.4 hours = 2.8 hours

Step 5: Convert the total time from hours to minutes.
- 1 hour = 60 minutes
- Total time in minutes = 2.8 hours × 60 minutes/hour = 168 minutes
