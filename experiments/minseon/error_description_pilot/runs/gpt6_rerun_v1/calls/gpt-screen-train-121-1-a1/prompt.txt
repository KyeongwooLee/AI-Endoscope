QUESTION: Stella and Twinkle are filling up a truck with a capacity of 6000 stone blocks at the rate of 250 blocks per hour per person. They work for four hours and are then joined by 6 other people who also work at the same rate. How many hours did filling the truck take?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the number of blocks Stella and Twinkle can fill in the first 4 hours.
Stella's rate: 250 blocks per hour
Twinkle's rate: 250 blocks per hour
Total rate for Stella and Twinkle: 250 + 250 = 500 blocks per hour
Number of blocks in 4 hours: 500 * 4 = 2000 blocks

Step 2: Calculate the total number of blocks that can be filled by Stella, Twinkle, and the additional 6 people in the remaining time.
Total number of people: Stella + Twinkle + 6 others = 2 + 6 = 8 people
Rate of all people together: 250 blocks per hour per person * 8 people = 2000 blocks per hour
Number of blocks in the remaining time: 4 hours * 2000 blocks per hour = 8000 blocks

Step 3: Calculate the total number of blocks filled.
Total blocks filled: 2000 blocks (first 4 hours) + 8000 blocks (remaining time) = 10000 blocks

Step 4: Calculate the total time taken to fill the truck.
Total capacity of the truck: 6000 blocks
Total time: 10000 blocks / 6000 blocks per hour = 1.67 hours
