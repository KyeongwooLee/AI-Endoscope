QUESTION: American carmakers produce 5 650 000 cars each year. Then they distribute it to 5 car suppliers. The first supplier receives 1 000 000 cars each. The second supplier receives 500 000 cars more while the third car supplier receives the same number as the first and second suppliers combined. The fourth and the fifth supplier receive an equal number of cars. How many cars do the fourth and fourth suppliers each receive?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of cars distributed to the first three suppliers.
- First supplier: 1,000,000 cars
- Second supplier: 1,000,000 + 500,000 = 1,500,000 cars
- Total for first three suppliers: 1,000,000 + 1,500,000 = 2,500,000 cars

Step 2: Calculate the number of cars remaining for the fourth and fifth suppliers.
- Total cars: 5,650,000
- Cars for first three suppliers: 2,500,000
- Remaining cars: 5,650,000 - 2,500,000 = 3,150,000 cars

Step 3: Calculate the number of cars each of the fourth and fifth suppliers receive.
- Total cars for fourth and fifth suppliers: 3,150,000
- Each receives an equal number of cars: 3,150,000 / 2 = 1,575,000 cars
