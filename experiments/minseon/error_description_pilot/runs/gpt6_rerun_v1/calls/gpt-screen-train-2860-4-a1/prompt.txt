QUESTION: Mr. Mitchell is buying pizzas for the soccer team. He buys one slice for every goal they scored on the season. A large pizza has 12 slices. If he bought 6 pizzas, and the team had 8 games, how many goals did they score per game on average?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total number of slices Mr. Mitchell bought.
Total slices = Number of pizzas × Slices per pizza
Step 2: Calculate the total number of goals scored by the team.
Total goals = Total slices
Step 3: Calculate the average number of goals per game.
Average goals per game = Total goals / Number of games
#### 1
Explanation: Since each pizza has 12 slices and Mr. Mitchell bought 6 pizzas, the total number of slices is 6 pizzas × 12 slices/pizza = 72 slices. The team had 8 games, so the total number of goals scored by the team is 72 slices. To find the average number of goals per game, we divide the total number of goals by the number of games, which gives us 72 goals / 8 games = 9 goals per game.
