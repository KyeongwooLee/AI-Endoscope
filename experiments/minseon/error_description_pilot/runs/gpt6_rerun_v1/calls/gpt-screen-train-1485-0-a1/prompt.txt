QUESTION: Adam has 50 balls. 20 balls are red, 10 are blue, and the rest are either pink or orange. If there are 3 times as many pink as orange balls, how many orange balls are there?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Determine the total number of red and blue balls.

Step 2: Calculate the number of pink and orange balls.
Total balls = 50
Red and blue balls = 30
Pink and orange balls = 50 - 30 = 20

Step 3: Set up the equation for the relationship between pink and orange balls.
Let x be the number of orange balls.
3x = 20 (since there are 3 times as many pink as orange balls)

Step 4: Solve for x.
x = 20 / 3
