QUESTION: Andy walks 50 meters from his house to school. After school, he comes back to the house and goes to the market. If he walks 140 meters in total, how many meters is the distance between the house and the market?
SOLUTION: Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. Andy walks from his house to school, which is 50 meters.
2. After school, he comes back to the house, adding another 50 meters to his total.
3. Now, the total distance he walks is 140 meters.
4. To find the distance between the house and the market, we need to subtract the distance from his house to school (50 meters) from the total distance walked (140 meters).

Calculation:
\[ \text{Distance between house and market} = \text{Total distance walked} - \text{Distance from house to school} \]
\[ \text{Distance between house and market} = 140 \text{ meters} - 50 \text{ meters} \]
\[ \text{Distance between house and market} = 90 \text{ meters} \]

#### 90
The distance between the house and the market is 90 meters.
