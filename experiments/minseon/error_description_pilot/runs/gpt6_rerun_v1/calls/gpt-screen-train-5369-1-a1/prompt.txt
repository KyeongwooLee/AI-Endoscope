QUESTION: Matt has a peanut plantation that is 500 feet by 500 feet.  1 square foot of peanuts can make 50 grams of peanuts.  If it takes 20 grams of peanuts to make 5 grams of peanut butter and 1 kg of peanut butter sells for $10 how much does he make from his plantation?
SOLUTION: Step 1: Calculate the total area of the plantation.
Area = 500 feet × 500 feet = 250,000 square feet

Step 2: Calculate the total grams of peanuts produced.
Total grams of peanuts = 250,000 square feet × 50 grams per square foot = 12,500,000 grams

Step 3: Calculate the total grams of peanut butter produced.
Total grams of peanut butter = 12,500,000 grams / 20 grams = 625,000 grams

Step 4: Calculate the total kilograms of peanut butter produced.
Total kilograms of peanut butter = 625,000 grams / 1000 grams = 625 kilograms

Step 5: Calculate the total sales from peanut butter.
Total sales = 625 kilograms × $10 = $6,250
